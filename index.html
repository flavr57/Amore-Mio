GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program continues to welcome international applicants through qualifying investment funds and real estate in lower-density regions, while the D8 Digital Nomad Visa remains a streamlined option for remote workers earning above the minimum income threshold — making Portugal one of Europe's most accessible destinations for lifestyle relocations.

COST OF LIVING
Rents in Lisbon and Porto remain elevated compared to five years ago, though the market has shown signs of stabilization heading into mid-2026; the Silver Coast and interior Alentejo continue to offer meaningfully better value for expats who prioritize space, quiet, and quality of life over proximity to urban centers.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the refined successor to the original NHR — grants qualifying new residents a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-sourced professional income, with favorable treatment on many categories of foreign-sourced earnings, for up to ten years; it remains one of the most compelling fiscal arrangements available to newcomers anywhere in the EU.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Property prices in Lisbon and the Algarve remain firm, sustained by strong international demand, though some signs of softening are appearing in prime urban neighborhoods; buyers targeting secondary cities like Braga or Setúbal, or villages along the Silver Coast, are finding more accessible entry points without meaningful sacrifice in lifestyle.

If you are dreaming of a Portuguese garden draped in vivid cascades of color, know this: bougainvillea blooms most spectacularly when slightly stressed for water, so ease off irrigation once it is established and resist over-fertilizing with nitrogen, which feeds lush leaves at the direct expense of those luminous paper-thin bracts. At LAX and dozens of major airports across the U.S. and Europe, certified therapy dogs — golden retrievers, labs, and the occasional gentle giant — now roam departure terminals specifically to ease the anxiety of travelers before they board.
