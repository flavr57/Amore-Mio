GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa remains available through qualifying investment funds and venture capital — real estate routes in Lisbon and Porto are closed, but the program continues to draw international applicants seeking EU residency. Processing times have stabilized, and the pathway to citizenship after five years remains one of Europe's most accessible.
~
COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto continue to see above-average inflation in dining and services, driven partly by strong tourism demand and a growing expat community. Smaller cities — Braga, Évora, the Silver Coast — still offer a comfortable lifestyle at a fraction of comparable Western European capitals.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime, the successor to the original NHR program, offers a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-source income for qualifying new residents, with generous exemptions on most foreign-source income for up to ten years. Retirees and remote workers should confirm eligibility with a Portuguese tax advisor well before relocating.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Central Lisbon rentals now average €1,500–€2,500 per month — still well below London or Paris, but a notable climb over recent years. Buyers and renters targeting secondary cities or the interior continue to find attractive options at prices that feel like a different era entirely.

Bougainvillea is one of those plants that rewards tough love — reducing watering in peak summer actually triggers the mild stress response that pushes the plant into its most spectacular floral display. Along Portugal's Algarve coast or on a sun-drenched Lisbon hillside, established bougainvillea needs almost no supplemental irrigation, making it one of the most effortlessly rewarding choices for an expat garden.
