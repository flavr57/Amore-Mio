GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains open but no longer accepts residential real estate investments — qualifying routes now center on investment funds, job creation, and cultural contributions. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa, requiring proof of roughly €3,480/month in remote income, continues to attract international applicants and remains one of the more accessible pathways.
~
COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and the Algarve continue to see upward pressure on prices — particularly dining and services — though both remain meaningfully more affordable than comparable coastal California living. Porto and interior regions offer a considerably gentler cost curve for those with flexibility on location.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI program — the successor to the original NHR — offers eligible new residents a 20% flat tax on Portuguese-source income for up to 10 years, with exemptions on many foreign-source income streams. Qualifying categories include technology workers, researchers, and certain professionals, making it well worth a conversation with a cross-border tax adviser.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental inventory in Lisbon and Porto remains tight, with average rents continuing to climb year-over-year, though the government's Mais Habitação housing reforms are beginning to add supply. Those willing to look beyond the capital — toward the Silver Coast, Alentejo, or smaller Algarve towns — will find considerably more breathing room and better value.

Researchers have developed a new forensic technique using tiny DNA samples from a wide range of animal sources to pinpoint the hotspots and supply chains of illegal wildlife trafficking — one of the world's most lucrative criminal enterprises. It is a quiet revolution in conservation enforcement, giving investigators a biological map where once there was only silence.

The Portuguese Water Dog: A Loyal Atlantic Companion With Ancient Roots
Long before they became a beloved family breed, Portuguese Water Dogs worked alongside fishermen along the Algarve coast — herding fish into nets, retrieving lost tackle, and even carrying messages between boats — earning a permanent place in the culture of Portugal's Atlantic seaboard. For anyone planning a Portuguese chapter, this curly-coated companion is practically a cultural ambassador.
