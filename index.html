GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains open via qualifying fund investments, job creation, and cultural donations — the residential property route in Lisbon, Porto, and the Algarve closed in 2023, but fund-based pathways continue to attract global applicants. Processing has become more predictable, with approvals increasingly arriving within six to eight months.
~
COST OF LIVING
One-bedroom apartments in desirable Lisbon and Porto neighborhoods now average €1,200–1,600 per month, reflecting continued demand from remote workers and expats. The Silver Coast and interior Alentejo remain significantly more affordable while offering exceptional quality of life.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's updated IFICI regime — successor to the original NHR — offers new residents a 20% flat rate on Portuguese-source professional income and broad exemptions on foreign pension and investment income for up to 10 years. A local fiscal representative can ensure your application aligns with your specific income profile.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental demand in Lisbon continues to outpace supply, with landlords gravitating toward short-term and corporate leases. Buyers are finding more value in emerging neighborhoods like Mouraria and Marvila, where prices remain below the €4,000/m² levels common in Príncipe Real and Chiado.

Bougainvillea in Late Summer: How to Keep the Color Coming
Bougainvillea puts on its most spectacular show when slightly stressed — a little less water and maximum sun coax those bracts into their richest magentas and corals. In late July, resist high-nitrogen fertilizers, which push leafy green growth at the expense of blooms; a low-nitrogen, phosphorus-rich feed is exactly what your plant is asking for right now. In coastal climates especially, excellent drainage is non-negotiable — bougainvillea loves fierce sun but despises wet roots.
