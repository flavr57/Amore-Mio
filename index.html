GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's ARI (Golden Visa) remains open to qualifying investment fund contributions and real estate in low-density regions, with processing times steadily improving as the agency clears its backlog. Applications have rebounded in 2026, reflecting continued international confidence in Portugal as a long-term destination.

COST OF LIVING
Living costs in Lisbon and Porto continue to run ahead of the national average, though both cities remain noticeably more affordable than London, Paris, or Amsterdam. Groceries, dining, and transport stay reasonable — the pressure is felt most acutely in rent, particularly in central neighborhoods.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's updated IFICI regime — successor to the original NHR — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-sourced professional income, plus broad exemptions on many categories of foreign income, for a full 10-year period. New applications continue to be accepted, making this one of Europe's more competitive tax incentives for relocated professionals.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in Lisbon have plateaued at elevated levels while the Algarve sees sustained demand from Northern European and American buyers. Government housing measures have modestly cooled speculative activity, but supply remains tight in prime areas — early movers still hold a meaningful advantage.

Scientists Map the Underground Fungal Web That Quietly Holds Earth Together
A team of scientists dedicated to fungal conservation set out to measure the total extent of Earth's underground mycelial networks — and arrived at an almost incomprehensible answer: 62 quadrillion miles of fungal threads connecting forest floors around the planet. These networks shuttle nutrients between trees, signal stress across ecosystems, and underpin much of terrestrial life as we know it. The finding is a reminder that the most important infrastructure on Earth is the one we're only just beginning to see.

Bougainvillea's Secret to Spectacular Color: Sun, Stress, and Strategic Neglect
Bougainvillea blooms most extravagantly when it is slightly thirsty, planted in full blazing sun, and given very little nitrogen-heavy fertilizer — which pushes leaves at the expense of the vibrant bracts that look like petals but are actually modified leaves. These bracts intensify in color under mild drought stress, a survival signal the plant transforms into something spectacular. Whether on a Hermosa Beach fence or a future Portuguese terrace, the recipe is the same: plant it hot, water sparingly, and step back.

Therapy Dogs Help Reluctant Young Readers Find Their Voice and Confidence
Through the READ program — Reading Education Assistance Dogs — certified therapy dogs are placed in libraries and classrooms where children who struggle to read aloud can practice before a patient, utterly non-judgmental audience. The dogs don't correct, don't sigh, don't rush; they simply listen, and children who froze before teachers find themselves reading whole chapters with ease. Studies consistently show measurable gains in fluency — and more importantly, a transformed relationship with books that follows these children for life.
