260 Volunteers Restore England's Largest-Ever Oyster Reef Off Portsmouth
Last week, 260 volunteers gathered at Chichester Harbour near Portsmouth to deposit 20,000 oysters into the sea, recreating historic biodiverse reefs that had all but vanished from English waters. It is the largest oyster reef restoration effort England has ever undertaken, driven by a coalition of scientists, local communities, and marine enthusiasts. The new reefs will filter coastal waters naturally and provide sanctuary for dozens of marine species for generations to come.

GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa remains active via the investment fund route (€500K minimum), offering a reliable path to EU residency for non-EU nationals. AIMA has reported improved processing times in 2026 for complete applications, making this a strong window for those ready to move.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto remain meaningfully more affordable than most Western European capitals, though expat demand has pushed everyday costs up 10–15% over the past two years. The Algarve and the Silver Coast still offer genuine value for those willing to trade the capital's energy for sunshine and space.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime (NHR 2.0), in effect since January 2024, offers a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-sourced income and favorable treatment of foreign income for qualifying new residents over a 10-year period. It remains especially attractive for remote workers, retirees, and those with foreign pension or investment income.

HOUSING MARKET
A comfortable two-bedroom in popular Lisbon neighborhoods now runs €1,800–€2,500/month, and buyer competition near the capital remains fierce. Setúbal, Comporta, and the interior Alentejo offer beautiful alternatives at significantly lower prices — and increasingly, the savvier expats are finding them first.
