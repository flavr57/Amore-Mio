Portugal's Golden Visa remains accessible through qualifying investment funds, business creation, and cultural donations, while the D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to welcome remote workers who can demonstrate monthly income of at least €3,480. COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen steady price increases in recent years, though both cities remain meaningfully more affordable than London, Paris, or New York for everyday life. NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the successor to the original NHR — offers qualifying new residents a 20% flat tax on Portuguese-source income and potential exemptions on foreign income for up to 10 years. Those who secured NHR status before the 2024 transition remain grandfathered in for the full duration of their original benefit period.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental demand in Lisbon continues to outpace supply, keeping central-neighborhood rents elevated, though property prices have stabilized after years of rapid appreciation. Buyers and renters are finding better value just outside the capital — along the Estoril coast, in Setúbal, or on the Alentejo frontier.
