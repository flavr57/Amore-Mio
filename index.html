GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program continues to accept applications through investment funds, job creation, and cultural contributions, even after the 2023 closure of real estate routes. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa remains a popular and accessible pathway for remote workers earning at least €3,480/month, with processing times gradually improving.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen sustained rent increases in recent years, with urban apartments now commanding prices that surprised many early expats. The Alentejo, Silver Coast, and interior cities remain noticeably more affordable — and offer a quieter, deeply authentic Portuguese pace of life.

NHR TAX REGIME
The original NHR program closed to new applicants in early 2024 and was replaced by IFICI, which offers qualifying residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-source income for ten years. Many foreign pension and passive income streams may also remain partially exempt — a Portuguese fiscal advisor is essential to optimize the structure.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Portugal's property market remains competitive, with Lisbon and the Algarve seeing tight rental inventory and strong international demand. New government housing initiatives are slowly expanding supply, and secondary cities like Braga, Setúbal, and Évora are offering meaningfully better availability and value.

Bougainvillea's Secret: A Little Tough Love Unlocks the Most Vivid Blooms
Bougainvillea thrives on restrained care — slightly cramped roots, full sun, and deliberately limited watering actually trigger the most spectacular displays of papery, jewel-toned bracts. In coastal climates like Hermosa Beach (and Portugal's Algarve, where bougainvillea cascades over nearly every whitewashed wall), a south-facing surface turns these climbers into pure theatre. Feed monthly with a low-nitrogen, high-potassium fertilizer during the growing season and they will reward you with color that stops people mid-stride.
