GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program continues to offer EU residency pathways through qualifying investment funds, with applications from non-EU nationals remaining active and processing times gradually improving. The program has stabilized following the 2023 reforms that shifted the primary route away from direct real estate purchases toward approved fund investments.

In Japan, Ancient Superstition Quietly Reshapes the Real Estate Market
In Japan, a deeply rooted cultural aversion to homes where sudden or violent deaths have occurred creates a peculiar shadow market in real estate, with disclosure laws requiring sellers to reveal such histories to prospective buyers and tenants. Developers have even built smartphone apps to help people check whether a property carries what the Japanese call a 'stigma' — and the price discounts on these homes can be substantial. It's a fascinating window into how ancient belief systems quietly but powerfully reshape thoroughly modern economic behavior.

Homegrown Catnip Proves as Powerful as DEET Against Mosquitoes
Researchers presenting at an international science conference revealed that a lotion made from locally grown catnip plants is as effective as DEET at repelling mosquitoes — a finding with transformative potential for rural communities in Uganda and across sub-Saharan Africa. Because catnip can be cultivated at minimal cost almost anywhere, this discovery offers a sustainable, community-grown alternative to expensive commercial repellents. It's the kind of elegant, nature-forward solution that could meaningfully reduce the burden of malaria and other mosquito-borne illnesses for millions of people.

Spain's Gentle Donkeys Are Nature's Own Wildfire Prevention Squad
In the ancient Doñana National Park of southern Spain, a herd of hardworking donkeys has been doing what machinery cannot — quietly grazing down the dry grasses and scrub that fuel catastrophic wildfires, from dawn to dusk. During Europe's recent heat dome, when multiple Spanish regions were scorched, Doñana's donkey brigade held the line, their patient browsing creating natural firebreaks across a precious Iberian ecosystem. It's a beautiful reminder that some of the oldest solutions are also the wisest — and, it must be said, the most charming.
