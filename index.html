GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's reformed Golden Visa remains open through qualifying investment fund contributions and job-creation routes — real estate is largely off the table in most areas, but the pathway for investors is very much alive. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to welcome remote workers with a relatively straightforward application for those earning above the monthly income threshold.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen notable price increases in recent years, with groceries and dining up roughly 12–15% from pre-2024 levels — though still well below California costs by a comfortable margin. Venturing inland to towns like Évora, Viseu, or along the Silver Coast unlocks significantly more affordable daily living without sacrificing quality or beauty.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI program — the successor to the beloved NHR — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% income tax rate on Portuguese-sourced professional income for a full ten years. The regime is more targeted than the old NHR (particularly around foreign pensions), but remains a genuinely compelling incentive for tech professionals, researchers, and entrepreneurs making the move.

HOUSING MARKET
The rental market in Lisbon remains tight and competitive, with quality two-bedroom apartments in desirable neighborhoods running €1,800–€2,500 per month. The property purchase market is offering more options as interest rates ease slightly — and buying ahead of your move could lock in better pricing before continued demand pushes values further.

Rare Kentish Milkwort Makes a Stunning Sevenfold Comeback from Extinction
Conservationists in Britain are celebrating after the Kentish milkwort — a delicate and uniquely British wildflower — recorded a remarkable sevenfold population increase, pulling it decisively back from the brink of extinction. Years of dedicated habitat restoration and targeted seeding by passionate volunteers turned the tide for this exquisite little bloom. It is a reminder that when the right people refuse to give up on a species, nature has a way of meeting them halfway.

Nasturtiums Are the Cheerful, Edible Flower Your Garden Has Been Missing
Few plants reward the low-maintenance gardener quite like nasturtiums — sow them in poor soil, give them full sun, and they will cascade into a riot of orange, yellow, and red with almost no help from you. Both the flowers and the peppery leaves are fully edible, making them as useful in the kitchen as they are beautiful trailing over a balcony or terrace. For anyone planning a Portuguese garden, nasturtiums are an ideal starting point: they adore the Mediterranean climate and will self-seed generously year after year.

Researchers have confirmed that dogs possess a dedicated region of the brain specifically attuned to processing human voices — meaning the joy your dog shows at the sound of your footsteps or your name for them is as neurologically real as any emotion we experience ourselves.
