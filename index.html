COST OF LIVING
A one-bedroom apartment in central Lisbon now averages €1,400–1,800/month, reflecting steady demand from international arrivals and returning Portuguese diaspora. Everyday expenses — groceries, dining out, public transport — still run comfortably below most Western European capitals, and the Algarve and Silver Coast remain noticeably more affordable.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime (successor to the original NHR, introduced in 2024) offers qualifying new residents a 20% flat tax on Portuguese-sourced income for up to 10 years, with foreign pension and passive income often exempt under applicable tax treaties. Applications are filed through the Portuguese Tax and Customs Authority.

Hachiko Still Teaches Us What Devotion Looks Like, Ninety Years On
In 1925, an Akita named Hachiko began meeting his owner — Tokyo professor Hidesaburo Ueno — at Shibuya Station every evening without fail. When Ueno died suddenly of a cerebral hemorrhage, Hachiko returned to the station every single day for nearly ten years, through summer heat and autumn rain and the puzzled kindness of strangers, until his own death in 1935. A bronze statue stands at Shibuya Station today, not as a monument to grief, but to the quiet, unconditional faithfulness that dogs offer us — whether or not we've done anything to deserve it.
