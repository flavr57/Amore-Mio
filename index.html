GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains open through fund investments and scientific contributions — the direct real estate routes in Lisbon and Porto were closed in 2023, but the alternatives are active and processing times have improved in 2026. The D8 digital nomad visa continues to welcome remote workers who can demonstrate consistent monthly income above roughly €3,480.
~
COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto continue to see upward pressure on everyday prices, though both cities remain meaningfully more affordable than comparable Western European capitals like Amsterdam or Barcelona. Targeting local markets and less-touristed neighborhoods still makes for an excellent quality of life at a small fraction of what coastal California demands.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI tax incentive — the updated successor to the popular NHR scheme — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-source income and exemptions on most foreign-sourced income for up to ten years. For those arriving from high-tax environments like California, the cumulative savings can be very substantial.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental demand in Lisbon and Porto remains strong, with central apartments commonly running €1,500–€2,500 per month — elevated by Portuguese standards, though still well below comparable neighborhoods along the California coast. The Algarve and Silver Coast continue to offer better value for those open to a slightly quieter pace of life.
