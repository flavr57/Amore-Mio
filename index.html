NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI program — which replaced the old NHR scheme in January 2024 — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-source income and generous exemptions on most foreign income for a full 10 years. It remains one of Europe's most attractive tax frameworks for expats.
