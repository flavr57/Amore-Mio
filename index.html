GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa remains active through qualifying investment funds (€500k+) and innovation/research routes — the residential real estate pathway closed in late 2023, but fund-based options continue to grant residency and a clear path to citizenship. It remains one of Europe's most accessible programs for non-EU applicants.
~
COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto continue to feel upward price pressure as expat and tourism demand holds strong — groceries and dining still run well below California norms, but central rents have climbed 15–20% over two years. Interior towns like Évora, Viseu, and the Silver Coast remain meaningfully more affordable.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the successor to NHR — offers a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-source income for qualifying new residents, with favorable treatment for those in tech, research, and creative fields. Registration must happen within 60 days of establishing tax residency, so planning ahead matters.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental supply in Lisbon and Porto stays constrained, with central 2-bedroom apartments typically running €1,400–€2,000/month — high by Portuguese historical standards, though still well below comparable US coastal cities. The government's Mais Habitação housing program continues its push to ease pressure, with modest results so far.

Coaxing a Second Flush from Your Bougainvillea Before Summer Ends
If your bougainvillea has gone quiet after its spring blaze, a brief, deliberate stress is exactly what it needs: ease back on watering for two to three weeks until you see slight leaf droop, then resume — this triggers the plant's blooming response. Bougainvillea flowers on new growth, so a light post-flush trim combined with a low-nitrogen, high-phosphorus fertilizer will push another wave of color well into autumn. In Portugal's warm coastal climate, a well-tended bougainvillea can deliver three brilliant flushes in a single year.
