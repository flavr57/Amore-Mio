GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa remains open through the fund investment route (€500,000 minimum into approved venture or investment funds), offering a clear path to residency and eventual citizenship. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to attract remote workers, with processing times improving at most consulates heading into summer.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto continue to see modest upward pressure on everyday expenses in 2026, though both cities remain dramatically more affordable than coastal California. Smaller cities like Setúbal, Braga, and the Silver Coast offer a noticeably gentler cost profile for those open to exploring beyond the capitals.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the updated NHR 2.0 effective since January 2024 — offers eligible new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-source income and broad exemptions on qualifying foreign income for a full decade. For remote workers and retirees, it remains one of Europe's most compelling tax arrangements.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in Lisbon have stabilized somewhat following government rent-control measures, but quality inventory in central neighborhoods remains competitive and demand is strong. Buyers are finding more negotiating room outside the capital — particularly in the Alentejo and along the Silver Coast.
