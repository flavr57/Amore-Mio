GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains open for qualifying investments in venture funds, cultural production, and scientific research — real estate was removed in 2023, but the pathway is alive and well for those exploring alternatives. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to attract remote workers with its streamlined application and renewable 12-month stay.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto continue to see upward pressure on everyday costs — dining, groceries, and utilities — though both cities remain meaningfully more affordable than Paris, London, or Zurich. The Algarve and smaller inland towns still offer genuinely lower costs for those open to trading city buzz for sunlit tranquility.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's updated IFICI program (Incentivo Fiscal à Criatividade e Inovação), which replaced the old NHR scheme in 2024, offers a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-source income and significant exemptions on qualifying foreign income for up to 10 years. New residents who register before 2027 may still access favorable transitional provisions — worth a conversation with a local fiscal advisor.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in Lisbon's historic neighborhoods have stabilized somewhat following government rent-control measures, but demand remains high and quality long-term leases in desirable areas move quickly. Mid-sized cities like Setúbal and Braga continue to offer excellent lifestyle at a fraction of Lisbon prices — worth serious consideration for the value-minded mover.

Ireland Becomes the First Nation to Pay Artists a Basic Income
Ireland has quietly launched one of the most imaginative social programs in Europe: a guaranteed basic income for working artists, providing a steady stipend so creatives can focus on their craft without the constant undertow of financial precarity. The scheme rests on a simple and long-overdue idea — that artists enrich the cultural and economic fabric of society far beyond what the market typically rewards them for. Advocates across Europe are watching closely, hoping Ireland's lead will inspire other nations to rethink how imagination is valued.

Britain's Last Coal Mining Application Rejected — an Energy Era Ends
A Welsh council's refusal to approve plans for an 85,000-ton coal extraction project means the United Kingdom now has zero outstanding coal mining applications anywhere in the country — a remarkable milestone for the island nation that powered the Industrial Revolution on coal for two centuries. The decision reflects how thoroughly public and political sentiment has shifted toward renewable alternatives. It's a quiet but consequential chapter in Britain's long, irreversible goodbye to fossil fuels.

Bougainvillea at Peak Season: Keep the Color Blazing Through September
June is bougainvillea's golden hour — the long days and warm coastal nights trigger the most spectacular flowering of the year, and the key is knowing when to step back. Hold off on nitrogen-heavy fertilizers, which push lush green growth at the expense of those glorious papery bracts; a phosphorus-rich feed every two to three weeks will reward you with cascading color through the summer. If you're dreaming of a Portugal garden, take heart: bougainvillea practically grows itself in the Algarve's climate, draping whitewashed walls in magenta from spring to late autumn.

Lavender on the Windowsill: The Stress and Sleep Plant Worth Keeping Close
Few plants earn their keep quite like lavender — it asks for almost nothing (a sunny ledge, well-drained soil, and restraint with the watering can) and gives back in fragrance, calm, and measurable reductions in cortisol when kept in living spaces. Studies have linked lavender's linalool compounds to improved sleep quality and reduced anxiety, making a small pot by the bedroom window one of the most effortless wellness upgrades a plant parent can make. As a bonus, it's a staple of Portuguese cottage gardens — deeply at home in the light and heat that awaits you across the Atlantic.
