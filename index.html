It is, in every sense, a find that reminds you the ocean is still full of wonders.

Hiring Your Kids: When Family Payroll Is Smart Tax Strategy
When you run your own business and have teenagers old enough to do real work, putting them on payroll is one of the more elegant tools in the self-employed toolkit — you deduct their wages as a legitimate business expense, and they are taxed on that income at their own rate, which is almost always far lower than yours. The financial lift is real but approximate: a teenager with modest earned income and no other earnings may have much of it sheltered by their own standard deduction, turning dollars you would otherwise pay tax on at your top bracket into something close to zero-tax income for the family as a whole. In an unincorporated sole proprietorship, there is an additional kicker worth knowing about: wages paid to a child under a specific age may be exempt from FICA taxes under IRC Section 3121(b)(3), which means more of the money stays inside the family rather than flowing to the government. The honest catch is documentation — the IRS scrutinizes family employment arrangements closely, so job descriptions should describe real tasks, timesheets should be kept exactly as you would for any outside hire, and pay rates should reflect what the open market would charge for the same work. Since these rules have moved over the years and your entity type — sole prop, S-corp, LLC — meaningfully changes what applies, a CPA should sign off before you cut the first paycheck; think of that conversation as the cost of doing this right. To dig in, search IRS Publication 15 (Circular E), IRS Publication 929, and the phrase 'IRC Section 3121(b)(3)' to understand the age thresholds, structure requirements, and what the W-2 obligations look like. Underwater Umbrellas Could Shield Coral Reefs From Rising Heat
Florida scientists have found that temporary shade structures deployed over stressed coral — essentially underwater umbrellas — can protect reefs from heat-induced bleaching and may even help partially reverse bleaching that has already taken hold. The discovery is a rare piece of genuinely hopeful news for coral ecosystems that have been under severe thermal pressure, suggesting that targeted, low-tech interventions could buy reefs critical time while the larger work of reducing ocean temperatures continues. It is the kind of practical, ingenious science that makes you feel measurably better about what human ingenuity can do when it's pointed in the right direction.

October Is Prime Time to Push Your Bougainvillea Toward Its Best Bloom
As Hermosa Beach holds warm into October, bougainvillea is in its sweet spot — shortening days trigger a mild stress response the plant translates into a spectacular flush of color, which means now is exactly the moment to ease off watering and hold back on nitrogen-heavy fertilizer, both of which push leafy growth at the expense of bracts. A slightly dry, nutrient-lean autumn is counterintuitively what this plant is asking for, and the payoff over the next few weeks can be extraordinary against a clear blue sky. If you are thinking ahead to Portugal, where bougainvillea climbs every wall from the Algarve to Lisbon, consider taking a semi-hardwood cutting now to propagate — they root beautifully in warm weather and would make a wonderful living keepsake of home.

Three Plants That Genuinely Earn Their Place on a Bedroom Windowsill
If you want plants that work for you while you sleep, the short list is snake plant (Sansevieria), lavender, and pothos — three species backed by real research and centuries of practical use for air quality and calming effect. Snake plants are among the few that continue releasing oxygen and absorbing carbon dioxide through the night rather than pausing their gas exchange; lavender's scent has measurable effects on cortisol and sleep latency; and pothos handles low-light humidity regulation with almost no care. All three travel well — and all three will feel immediately at home in a Portuguese apartment with good Atlantic light.

Today Is World Animal Day — and Dogs Have Earned Every Bit of It
October 4th is World Animal Day, a tradition dating to 1925 that coincides with the feast of St. Francis of Assisi, patron saint of animals — and if any creature deserves a day, it is the one that has walked beside us for at least 15,000 years. Research published in recent years has confirmed what dog owners have always quietly known: even ten minutes of interaction with a dog produces measurable drops in cortisol and meaningful rises in oxytocin, the bonding hormone, in both human and animal. It is one of the longest and most successful relationships in the history of the living world, and today is as good a day as any to feel grateful for it.
