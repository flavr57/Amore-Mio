GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program continues to accept applications through investment funds, qualifying capital transfers (€500,000+), and job-creation routes. Real estate no longer qualifies, but fund-based pathways remain popular with applicants seeking EU residency and a road to eventual citizenship.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto continue to see above-average inflation in food, dining, and services, narrowing the gap with other Western European capitals. The Algarve and smaller inland cities still offer considerably lower day-to-day costs for expats willing to look beyond the main hubs.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — successor to the beloved NHR — offers a flat 20% income tax rate on Portuguese-sourced income for qualifying new residents for up to 10 years. Foreign pension and investment income treatment has shifted, making an early conversation with a Portuguese tax advisor essential before you land.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in Lisbon and Porto remain elevated, with demand from both expats and domestic renters keeping supply tight. The Silver Coast and Alentejo offer considerably more breathing room, and many relocating Americans find their dollars go significantly further just an hour outside the capital.

The Carbon-Pricing Blueprint the World Copied Now Faces Its Hardest Test
The European Union's Emissions Trading System — the world's first major carbon market and a model since replicated by dozens of countries — is under renewed pressure as industry groups push back against stricter climate targets. The ETS works by capping total emissions and requiring companies to buy permits for what they release, creating a genuine financial incentive to go green rather than just a regulatory obligation. Its fate matters far beyond Europe: if the world's most-watched carbon-pricing experiment softens, it could slow global momentum toward putting a real price on pollution.

An Italian court handed the former head of the country's motorway operator a 12-year prison sentence for his role in the 2018 Morandi Bridge collapse in Genoa, which killed 43 people and exposed decades of deferred maintenance on critical public infrastructure. The case has reverberated across Europe as a stark reminder of what happens when accountability for public safety is allowed to quietly erode.

Astronomers Detect a Raspberry-Linked Sugar Drifting Through the Milky Way
Scientists have confirmed the detection of glycolaldehyde — a simple sugar also found in raspberries — floating in the gas and dust clouds near the center of our galaxy, marking the first time a sugar molecule has been identified in interstellar space. The discovery adds to growing evidence that the chemical building blocks of life are not rare accidents but common features of the cosmos, assembled in the cold between stars long before any planet forms. It's the kind of finding that makes the universe feel less empty — and somehow, a little sweeter.

Maine's River Herring Are Back — Over 20 Million Counted This Season
Counters on Maine's rivers recorded more than 20 million river herring during this year's annual spawning run — the highest number in decades, and a dramatic reversal for a fish that was illegal to harvest as recently as 2012. The recovery followed the removal of aging dams that had blocked migration routes for generations, allowing the fish to reach their ancestral spawning grounds again. It's a quiet, patient conservation success story: remove the obstacle, and life finds its way back.

Why This Week's Heat Is the Best Thing for Your Bougainvillea
With Hermosa Beach hitting the mid-80s this week, your bougainvillea is entering its happiest season — the plant produces its most extravagant color when it's slightly root-stressed and basking in at least six hours of direct sun. Resist the urge to overwater; those brilliant papery bracts (which are modified leaves, not petals) are triggered by dry spells between waterings rather than constant moisture. A light feed of low-nitrogen, high-phosphorus fertilizer this week will encourage a second vivid flush of color through late summer.

Screwworm Season Is Here — A Simple Checklist to Keep Your Dog Safe
Veterinary experts are flagging heightened awareness of screwworm flies this summer — parasitic insects whose larvae can infest open wounds on pets and cause serious harm if missed in the early stages. The defense is straightforward: check your dog's skin and any cuts regularly, keep wounds clean when outdoors, and visit your vet promptly if you notice unusual discharge or your dog is pawing at a wound that won't settle. A little daily attention goes a long way — and a dog who gets that kind of care has a very good human indeed.
