NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's updated IFICI benefit — the successor to the original NHR scheme — grants qualifying new residents a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-sourced income and broad exemptions on most foreign income for a full ten-year period. It remains one of the most competitive tax frameworks for international arrivals anywhere in Western Europe.

The Laukiai Oak of Lithuania — an ancient, cathedral-canopied giant that villagers have cherished for centuries and literally protected with a hand-built stone wall — has been named European Tree of the Year 2026. Nagaland's Tortoise Guardians Are Bringing Asia's Largest Land Tortoise Back
In the remote forests of Nagaland in northeastern India, communities who once hunted the giant Asian forest tortoise — mainland Asia's largest land tortoise — have transformed into its most passionate protectors. Working alongside government conservation programs, these self-appointed Tortoise Guardians are helping a critically endangered species inch back from the brink, turning a cultural shift into measurable ecological recovery. It is conservation at its most human: a community choosing, generation by generation, to rewrite its own story.

Ground Cover Plants That Out-Compete Weeds So You Never Pull One Again
The secret to a low-maintenance garden isn't a better hoe — it's choosing plants that do the work for you by creating a dense, living mulch that shades the soil and starves weed seeds of the light they need to germinate. This season, consider creeping thyme, hardy geraniums, or epimedium: all three are virtually indestructible, genuinely beautiful in their own right, and quietly relentless at keeping uninvited guests at bay. Plant them once, stand back, and let the garden take care of itself.
