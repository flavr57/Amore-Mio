Germany now faces potential European Union penalties after missing the deadline to incorporate the bloc's Pay Transparency Directive into national law — a measure that requires companies to open up their salary structures so employees can identify and challenge inequitable pay. The irony is pointed: Germany, Europe's largest economy and a self-styled beacon of labor standards, has stalled on one of the EU's most consequential social equity reforms. The case will test whether Brussels is willing to enforce its own progressive agenda against its most powerful member.

Bougainvillea's Paradox: A Little Stress Unlocks Its Most Spectacular Blooms
If your bougainvillea is generous with green growth but stingy with its vivid bracts, the counterintuitive fix is to stress it slightly — reduce watering to once every ten days or so and ensure it receives at least six hours of direct sun daily. The plant's blooming mechanism is triggered by mild drought, which is why it thrives so spectacularly along Mediterranean coastlines and, soon enough for you, on terraces all across Portugal. Think of it as the garden's reminder that some of the most beautiful things flourish precisely when conditions get a little demanding.
