Why Mortgage REITs Like AGNC and Annaly Pay So Generously
Mortgage REITs — AGNC Investment Corp. and Annaly Capital Management are the two largest in the U.S. — borrow capital at short-term rates and deploy it into pools of mortgage-backed securities, pocketing the spread between what they earn and what they owe. That spread, amplified by significant leverage, is what makes the yields so eye-catching: historically, mREIT dividends have run somewhere in the 8–14% range, though that figure is not stable and swings widely with the interest rate environment. The structural mechanism that forces such generous distributions is baked into REIT law — these companies are required to pass at least 90% of their taxable income to shareholders, so the cash flows out rather than compounding inside the business. The honest catch is that book value and the dividend itself are tightly tethered to the spread between short- and long-term rates; when the yield curve compresses or inverts, dividends can be cut sharply and share prices can fall with them. A concrete next step is to pull AGNC's and Annaly's investor relations pages side by side and compare their leverage ratios, portfolio duration, and the full dividend histories across the last two rate cycles — that history tells you far more than the current yield alone. The European Commission has tabled a sweeping set of reforms designed to make enlargement more workable: candidate countries would gain earlier access to the bloc's single market, voting rules would shift to reduce individual-member vetoes, and new safeguards would protect existing members during transitions. Canada Pauses Its Plan to Extend Assisted Dying to Mental Illness Cases
Canada has suspended — indefinitely — its plan to extend medical assistance in dying to people whose sole underlying condition is a mental illness, a step that had been scheduled to take effect in March 2027. The pause follows years of intense debate among clinicians, ethicists, and patient advocates about whether existing safeguards are sufficient to protect vulnerable people from irreversible decisions. The decision keeps Canada at the leading edge of end-of-life policy, where the questions now being wrestled with will likely shape legislation in other countries for decades.

France Suspends Stun Grenades After Teenager Loses Hand at Student Protest
The French government has halted use of stun grenades after a teenage boy's hand was blown off during student protests, following sustained pressure from civil-liberties groups alarmed at what they described as disproportionate force being used against minors. France has faced scrutiny over its crowd-control arsenal for years — the same grenades have injured protesters in previous demonstrations — and the pause represents a rare official acknowledgment that current practice requires review. Whether the suspension hardens into permanent reform, or prompts a broader rethink of protest policing across Europe, will be closely watched.

October Is the Quiet Sweet Spot for Repotting Your Orchids
As days shorten and temperatures ease, phalaenopsis orchids naturally slow their growth — which makes this the ideal window to repot any plant whose roots are escaping the container or whose bark medium has broken down to mush. Fresh orchid bark mix, a pot one size up, and a clean trim of any dead or rotting roots is all it takes; the plant will settle in comfortably before pushing its next flower spike. If yours is already in bud or in bloom, leave it in peace and repot after the flowers drop.

The Quietly Extraordinary Work Therapy Dogs Do in Hospital Corridors
Across hundreds of hospitals in the United States, trained therapy dogs and their handlers make regular rounds through oncology wards, pediatric units, and surgical recovery floors — and researchers have consistently documented measurable drops in patient anxiety, perceived pain, and even blood pressure following short visits. The dogs themselves seem to understand when to be still; experienced therapy animals will often settle gently beside a patient without any guidance, as though reading the room on their own. It is one of those interspecies partnerships that makes the world feel, at least for a few minutes, exactly as it should.
