What the American Opportunity Tax Credit Can Do for a College-Bound Kid
The American Opportunity Tax Credit is a federal tax credit — not a deduction — that directly reduces what you owe the IRS during the first four years of a dependent's higher education, which makes it worth thinking about now if your 18-year-old is college-bound. Historically it has run at up to roughly $2,500 per eligible student per year, and around 40% of it is refundable, meaning you can receive real cash back even in a year where your tax liability runs low. The mechanism is what makes it compelling: qualified tuition and course-related expenses trigger the credit, and because it cuts straight off your final bill rather than merely reducing taxable income, it carries more weight than a deduction of equivalent size. The honest complexity for a self-employed owner is that the credit phases out above certain modified adjusted gross income thresholds, so a strong revenue year can quietly shrink it — and because these rules shift and the interaction with other education benefits can get layered fast, this is genuinely the kind of thing to run past a CPA before assuming it applies to your situation. For your research, pull up IRS Form 8863 (the credit form itself) alongside IRS Publication 970, 'Tax Benefits for Education' — those two together map exactly what qualifies as an expense and where the income limits currently sit. China Switches On the World's Largest Liquid-Air Clean Energy Storage System
China has activated the world's largest liquid-air energy storage system — a technology that chills air into liquid form when power is abundant and cheap, then releases it as an expanding gas to drive turbines when demand peaks. Unlike conventional battery storage, it scales to grid size without depending on scarce minerals, which makes it one of the more promising approaches to the long-duration storage challenge at the heart of the clean energy transition. It's the kind of infrastructure milestone that rarely dominates headlines but quietly changes the math on how much renewable power a modern grid can reliably carry.

November Is the Perfect Moment to Set Your Bougainvillea Up for Spring
In a mild coastal climate like Hermosa Beach — or the sun-drenched south of Portugal, for that matter — bougainvillea enters a natural rest period in November after its summer flowering flush, and this pause is actually an opportunity. Hold back on water and fertilizer now to stress the plant just slightly, which is precisely the trigger that sets off its dramatic bloom burst in late winter and early spring. Resist any urge to prune until after that bloom, and you'll be rewarded with one of the most theatrical flowering displays any garden can offer.
