GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa remains available through qualifying routes including venture capital funds, job creation, and cultural heritage investment — the real estate pathway closed in 2023, but fund-based options are gaining traction with international applicants. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to attract remote workers earning roughly four times Portugal's minimum wage, with a smooth application process through the nearest consulate.
~
COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen meaningful price increases over the past two years, with groceries and dining inching closer to Western European norms — though both cities remain substantially more affordable than London, Paris, or Amsterdam. The Silver Coast and smaller inland towns still offer excellent day-to-day value for those willing to explore beyond the major urban centres.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's original NHR was replaced by the IFICI scheme (informally called NHR 2.0) for new applicants from 2024, offering a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-source income and broad exemptions on qualifying foreign income for up to ten years. Professionals in technology, science, and creative fields are among the eligible categories — a Portuguese tax advisor can confirm your classification before you apply.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in Lisbon's most desirable neighborhoods remain elevated, and the government's recent housing reform package has delivered mixed early results in cooling demand. Porto and the Setúbal Peninsula offer more breathing room for newcomers, and longer-term leases negotiated directly with landlords — rather than through short-let platforms — continue to yield the best rates for incoming residents.
