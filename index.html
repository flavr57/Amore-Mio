GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa remains open through fund subscriptions, job creation, and cultural investment routes, with the D8 Digital Nomad Visa offering a popular parallel pathway for remote workers. Application processing times through the AIMA agency have improved steadily through 2026, making the overall journey more predictable for prospective applicants.
~
COST OF LIVING
Portugal remains significantly more affordable than most of Western Europe — a comfortable lifestyle in Lisbon or Porto typically runs 60–70% of equivalent costs in London or Paris. That said, dining out and rental prices have risen meaningfully since 2022, so budget with current figures rather than those from pre-pandemic expat guides.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
The IFICI regime (Portugal's NHR 2.0, in effect since 2024) grants qualifying new residents a flat 20% income tax rate for ten years, with generous exemptions on most foreign-source income including pensions and passive investment returns. Retirees and remote workers with non-Portuguese income frequently find their effective tax burden drops dramatically under this framework.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in Lisbon and Porto have softened slightly from their 2024–2025 peaks but remain elevated, with well-located two-bedroom apartments in Lisbon typically ranging from €1,500 to €2,500 per month. The Algarve, Silver Coast, and smaller inland cities continue to offer significantly better value for quality of life.

Bougainvillea Blooms Best Under Pressure: Unlock Its Color with Calculated Drought
If your bougainvillea has gone quiet this summer, the most likely culprit is kindness — specifically, too much water. Bougainvillea blooms in response to mild stress: hold back irrigation for two to three weeks, ensure it's receiving at least six hours of full sun daily, and resist nitrogen-heavy fertilizers that push leafy green growth at the expense of color. The reward for this calculated tough-love approach is a cascade of vivid magenta or coral bracts that will carry you through August and well into fall.
