NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the 2024 successor to the beloved NHR — offers qualifying new residents a 20% flat tax on Portuguese-sourced income for up to 10 years. Foreign pension and select passive income categories may also benefit, keeping Portugal among Europe's most tax-welcoming destinations for incoming residents.

After 300 years of private family ownership, the dramatic volcanic Bass Rock — home to the world's largest Northern Gannet colony — has been sold to the Royal Society for the Protection of Birds, securing permanent sanctuary for roughly 100,000 gannets and 10,000 puffins. The decision by the longtime owners to prioritize conservation over private development is a rare and quietly extraordinary act of generational stewardship. It is, as the RSPB rightly called it, a wildlife wonder of the world — and now it will stay that way.

Bougainvillea at Peak Season: The Counterintuitive Secret to Maximum Summer Bloom
As June days lengthen and temperatures rise along the Southern California coast, bougainvillea enters its prime flowering window — and the counterintuitive key to spectacular color is deliberate stress: cut back watering significantly once buds begin to form, because drought pressure pushes the plant to channel its energy into vivid bracts rather than leafy growth. Pair that with a south-facing spot, at least six hours of direct sun, excellent drainage, and a monthly low-nitrogen fertilizer, and you'll be rewarded with cascading color well into autumn. In both Hermosa Beach and Portugal, bougainvillea is the plant that most perfectly defines what a sun-drenched summer wall looks like.
