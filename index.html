GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains open to qualifying investment fund and venture capital contributions, with processing times improving to under 12 months for many applicants. The program continues to offer a clear pathway to EU residency and eventual citizenship for those navigating its updated post-2024 framework.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen sustained price increases, with expats reporting monthly costs of roughly €2,000–€3,000 for a comfortable lifestyle outside city centers. The Alentejo, Braga, and smaller interior towns remain significantly more affordable — a compelling alternative for those whose income travels with them.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI program — the successor to the much-loved NHR scheme — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-sourced income and potential exemptions on foreign income for up to 10 years. Applications must be filed within the first year of establishing tax residency, making early planning essential.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in central Lisbon and Porto remain elevated, with well-located two-bedroom apartments averaging €1,800–€2,500 per month, though the Algarve and cities like Évora or Setúbal offer considerably more space for less. Government housing initiatives are slowly adding supply, but international demand continues to set the pace.

Spain's Grazing Donkeys Are Quietly Becoming the Nation's Best Wildfire Defense
In the ancient, sun-drenched ecosystem of Doñana National Park, a herd of donkeys has been put to work doing what donkeys do best — grazing — with the quietly revolutionary effect of reducing the dry brush that fuels devastating wildfires. It is perhaps the most low-tech, high-wisdom conservation story of the season: the Iberian landscape protecting itself, one mouthful at a time.

Give Your Bougainvillea Tough Love Now for Maximum Summer Colour
If your bougainvillea looks lush and leafy but disappointingly sparse on those brilliant magenta bracts, the fix is counterintuitive: water less. These drought-adapted beauties bloom most extravagantly when mildly stressed — let the soil dry out completely between waterings, and swap any nitrogen-rich fertilizer for a high-phosphorus blend to encourage flowering over foliage. In coastal Southern California — and soon, perhaps, along the sun-warmed garden walls of Portugal — a little strategic neglect is the secret to a spectacular show.
