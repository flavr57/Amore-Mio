GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program continues to attract international investors, with fund-based investment routes remaining the primary qualifying pathway since real estate was removed. The program has stabilized under clearer EU compliance guidelines, making it a reliable long-term residency option for non-EU applicants.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto remain significantly more affordable than comparable Western European capitals, though prices have risen noticeably over the past two years due to tourism demand and expat inflows. The Algarve and interior regions like the Alentejo still offer exceptional value for those willing to explore beyond the major cities.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's updated IFICI regime (the successor to NHR) offers a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-sourced income and favorable treatment of foreign income for qualifying new residents for 10 years. Applicants who registered under the original NHR before its 2024 sunset continue to enjoy their full benefits.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in Lisbon have softened slightly in early 2026 as new housing supply enters the market, though demand remains strong and availability tight in central neighborhoods. Buyers are finding more negotiating room in Porto and the Silver Coast, where inventory has grown relative to 2024 peaks.

Cooking at Home Once a Week May Cut Dementia Risk by 30%
A compelling new study has found that seniors who prepare home-cooked meals at least once a week may reduce their risk of dementia by 30% — and for those picking up cooking later in life, the benefit may be even more dramatic, with a 70% reduction observed among novice cooks. It's one of the loveliest prescriptions imaginable: more time in the kitchen, more meals made with care.

Bougainvillea in Coastal Gardens: How to Coax the Most Vibrant Blooms
Bougainvillea thrives in the kind of bright, sun-drenched conditions that Hermosa Beach and the Portuguese Algarve share so generously — but it blooms most extravagantly when mildly stressed, so resist the urge to overwater or over-fertilize. Allow the soil to dry out between waterings and give it a phosphorus-rich feed in spring to encourage those spectacular papery bracts in magenta, coral, and orange. Planted against a whitewashed wall, it's the very picture of Mediterranean summer.
