GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program continues under its revised framework, with qualifying routes including fund subscriptions from €500,000 and capital transfers of €1.5M — residential real estate in Lisbon and Porto no longer qualifies. The program remains a well-trodden pathway to EU residency and eventual citizenship for non-EU nationals, with processing times gradually improving.
~
COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen sustained price increases in dining, services, and groceries over the past two years, though everyday costs remain meaningfully lower than comparable Western European capitals. The Algarve and Portugal's interior regions continue to offer excellent value, and a thoughtful budget still goes a long way outside the city centres.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI scheme — the successor to the original NHR — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-source income for up to 10 years, along with potential exemptions on certain foreign-source income. Engaging a Portuguese tax adviser well before establishing residency remains strongly advisable to structure your situation correctly from the start.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental demand in Lisbon and Porto remains robust, keeping prices elevated, though a gradual rise in supply is beginning to ease pressure in outer neighbourhoods and commuter towns. The property purchase market stays competitive, with international buyers particularly active in Cascais, the Silver Coast, and the Algarve — early engagement with a buyer's agent pays dividends.
