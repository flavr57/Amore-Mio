COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto continue to see moderate price increases for groceries and dining, though the pace of inflation has slowed meaningfully compared to recent years. NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the successor to the original NHR — offers eligible new residents a 20% flat tax on Portuguese-sourced income for up to ten years, with favorable treatment for foreign pension and passive income. Americans qualifying as highly skilled professionals or retirees find it one of Europe's most compelling personal tax structures.
