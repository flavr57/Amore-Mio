GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program continues operating in 2026 through qualifying investment pathways — venture capital funds, job creation, and cultural contributions — following the removal of direct residential real estate. Processing times have improved, and the program remains one of Europe's more accessible routes to residency for non-EU applicants.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto remain meaningfully more affordable than Paris, Amsterdam, or London, though both cities have seen sustained price pressure over the past several years. Groceries, dining, and transport still offer excellent value — it is rent in expat-favored neighborhoods where the budget stretches thinnest.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the successor to the original NHR — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-sourced income for up to ten years, with favorable treatment for certain foreign income. It is more targeted than its predecessor but remains a genuinely compelling incentive, particularly for remote workers and professionals in innovation fields.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Stricter short-term rental regulations have brought modest stabilization to Lisbon's historic-center rents, though prices remain elevated relative to local wages. Buyers in 2026 find more inventory than the 2022–2023 peak offered, but desirable Algarve coastal properties and Lisbon riverfront neighborhoods still command strong premiums.

Bougainvillea Blooms Best When You Step Back and Let It Struggle
That riot of magenta and coral draped over every Portuguese wall worth photographing? It thrives on mild neglect. Overwatering and excess fertilizer push bougainvillea into lush foliage at the expense of flowers — the plant blooms most spectacularly when the soil dries out between waterings and nitrogen is kept low. In late summer, ease off irrigation entirely for two to three weeks to stress-trigger a bloom cycle that will carry you through autumn.
