The Account Where Your Income Grows and Leaves Untaxed
A Roth IRA is a retirement account funded with after-tax dollars, meaning every dollar of growth inside it — dividends, interest, capital gains — compounds without generating a tax bill, and qualified withdrawals in retirement come out entirely free of federal income tax. For income investors, this matters enormously: a dividend ETF or a high-yield bond fund holding that might historically generate distributions in a rough 3–6% range keeps every cent of that yield compounding inside the Roth, rather than triggering ordinary income tax year after year as it would in a taxable brokerage account. The mechanism is clean — because contributions are after-tax, the IRS effectively steps aside and lets the account grow uninterrupted, which is why income-heavy holdings make particularly good tenants inside this kind of wrapper. The trade-off is that annual contribution limits are modest and income-based phase-outs apply; since the rules and specific figures change periodically, it is worth confirming your current eligibility and contribution room with a CPA before making assumptions. A concrete thread to pull: compare how Vanguard, Fidelity, and Schwab each structure their Roth IRA platforms — look specifically at their income-focused fund options (high-yield bond, dividend equity, real asset funds) and think through what a hypothetical 20- or 30-year tax-free compounding scenario could look like. Tomorrow, we turn to asset location — the question of which income holdings actually belong in a Roth versus a taxable account, because the answer might surprise you.

Scientists Sequence a Whale's Full Genome Using Only Its Breath
Researchers have used drones to capture the exhaled mist from a whale's blowhole — and that single breath contained enough genetic material to complete the first full genome sequencing of one of these ocean giants without any physical intrusion whatsoever. No nets, no biopsies, no stress to the animal — just a drone hovering gently above the surface as the whale came up for air. It opens a door to understanding whale biology, disease, and evolution at a depth that was simply impossible before, entirely on the animal's own terms.

Bougainvillea in November: Coaxing a Second Flush Along the Coast
November along the Southern California coast is bougainvillea's quiet season — the summer spectacle has faded, but with a little intention now, a second round of color before year's end is genuinely within reach. The key is a mild, deliberate stress: ease back on watering for two to three weeks (bougainvillea blooms most lavishly when slightly drought-stressed), and switch from nitrogen-heavy fertilizer to a low-nitrogen, high-phosphorus blend to encourage flower set rather than leaf growth. In a garden with Hermosa Beach's mild winters, the plant may never fully go dormant — which means the window for late-season color stays open longer here than almost anywhere else on the continent.

The One Seed-Starting Habit That Separates Thriving Seedlings From Struggling Ones
The difference between seedlings that sprint ahead and ones that sulk often comes down to a single overlooked step: ensuring the growing medium is genuinely moist before seeds are sown, rather than watered after the fact. Expert grower Sally Nex points out that pre-moistened compost gives seeds immediate, even contact with water from the moment they germinate, while top-watering can compact the surface and wash seeds toward the edges of the tray. Getting this right at the start dramatically improves germination rates — and sets you up for the kind of strong, even seedlings that make transplanting a pleasure rather than a triage operation.

The Labrador Who Covered His Unconscious Owner and Went for Help
Endal, a golden Labrador Retriever and service dog in Hampshire, England, became famous for an act of pure, instinctive care: when his owner Allen Parton — a Gulf War veteran who had lost his speech and much of his memory — was knocked from his wheelchair and lay unconscious, Endal pulled a blanket over him, retrieved his mobile phone, and went to alert a nearby hotel for assistance.
