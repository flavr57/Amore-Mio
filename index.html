GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa remains open through qualifying investment fund and job-creation routes — property purchases in Lisbon and Porto no longer qualify, but the fund pathway is active and well-traveled by expats. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to attract remote workers with a straightforward application and a renewable two-year residency.
~
COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen noticeable inflation in everyday expenses, with groceries and dining out creeping closer to Northern European norms — though both cities remain meaningfully more affordable than London, Paris, or Southern California. The Silver Coast and inland Alentejo offer a considerably gentler cost profile for those with flexibility on location.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's original Non-Habitual Resident scheme has been succeeded by IFICI, which extends favorable flat-rate income tax treatment to qualifying professionals in tech, research, and higher education for up to ten years. Those who registered under the old NHR before the 2024 cutoff retain their full benefits — worth confirming with a Portuguese tax adviser if you haven't already.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Quality two-bedroom rentals in Lisbon's most coveted neighborhoods — Príncipe Real, Chiado, Estrela — routinely command €2,000–€2,800 per month, with inventory moving quickly. Looking slightly further afield to Mouraria, Alcântara, or Cascais can yield considerably better value without sacrificing the lifestyle that drew you there.

Bougainvillea Secrets: Coaxing More Blooms With One Simple Trick
Bougainvillea blooms on stressed wood, not pampered growth — which means the most reliable way to trigger a spectacular flush of those papery bracts is to hold back on watering until the leaves just begin to droop, then water deeply and watch the color ignite. In Portugal's warm, dry summers this cycle happens almost naturally, which is one reason bougainvillea thrives there as if born for the place. Give yours full sun, lean soil, and a little tough love.
