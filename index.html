GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa remains active via investment funds, venture capital, and cultural or scientific projects — real estate no longer qualifies, but the pathway is clear and well-established for eligible investors. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to attract remote workers with its 12-month renewable structure and straightforward income threshold.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen meaningful price increases over the past two years, with groceries and dining still substantially below comparable US coastal cities like Hermosa Beach. The Algarve and Silver Coast remain more affordable alternatives, particularly for those willing to settle slightly outside tourist-heavy centers.

NHR TAX REGIME
The new IFICI regime — successor to the original NHR — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-source income and broad exemptions on most foreign-source income for up to 10 years. Early engagement with a Portuguese tax advisor is worthwhile, as qualifying professions and income categories differ somewhat from the old NHR framework.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in Lisbon and Porto remain elevated, with demand outpacing supply in central neighborhoods — though new residential development is gradually expanding inventory. Buyers are finding better value outside the capital, with the Setúbal Peninsula and the Alentejo coast drawing growing interest from international purchasers.

Bougainvillea is the signature summer spectacle across Portugal's whitewashed walls — and in Hermosa Beach's mild coastal climate, it's equally at home right now. To maximize blooms, keep it slightly stressed: resist overwatering (drought encourages flowering over foliage), give it full sun, and hold off on nitrogen-heavy fertilizers that push leaves at the expense of color. A well-placed bougainvillea is low-maintenance drama — exactly the kind of plant that travels well in spirit from one sunny shore to another.
