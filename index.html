GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains open for qualifying investment routes — including venture capital funds, cultural contributions, and job-creation projects — following the removal of direct real estate in 2023. AIMA has continued to improve its digital processing pipeline in 2026, with applicants reporting somewhat faster timelines than in prior years.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto continue to experience above-average inflation in food and services, though daily life remains roughly 30–40% less expensive than comparable US coastal cities. Smaller towns along the Silver Coast and inland Alentejo offer noticeably more purchasing power for expats willing to trade big-city energy for quiet and space.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the successor to the original NHR — continues to offer qualifying new residents a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-sourced income for up to ten years. Foreign pension and passive income treatment is more nuanced than the old NHR, so a conversation with a Portuguese fiscal advisor before applying remains strongly advisable.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in Lisbon's most desirable neighborhoods have stabilized somewhat after years of steep climbs, though quality inventory remains tight and competition is real. Buyers in the €300,000–€600,000 range are finding better value in Porto's newer districts and the Algarve's secondary towns, where international demand is growing but has not yet overtaken supply.

Archaeologists Find a Stonehenge Prototype Perfectly Aligned With the Solstice
Just days after the summer solstice, archaeologists in Britain have announced a once-in-a-career find: a prehistoric structure that appears to be an early prototype for Stonehenge, with post holes and deliberate layout oriented to the solstice sunrise in a way that mirrors the great monument itself. Bougainvillea and the Art of Thriving on Benign Neglect
If there is one plant perfectly suited to a sun-drenched California terrace — or a future Portuguese courtyard — it is bougainvillea, which ranks among the toughest and most rewarding of all flowering plants. Once established, it requires almost no water, actively thrives in poor, well-drained soil, and rewards a firm annual prune with months of spectacular color. The trick most gardeners miss: bougainvillea blooms most extravagantly when it is slightly stressed, so resisting the urge to over-water or over-fertilize is the single greatest gift you can give it.

Clever Vertical Layering Can Make Any Small Garden Feel Expansive
Whether you are working with a Hermosa Beach patio or imagining a compact Portuguese courtyard, the principles for making a small outdoor space feel generous are remarkably consistent. Layering plants at three distinct heights — low groundcovers, medium flowering shrubs, and a single vertical accent like a trained jasmine or climbing rose — draws the eye upward and outward, dissolving the sense of enclosure. Pale-colored walls, diagonal planting lines, and a single well-placed mirror are among the simplest interventions that can genuinely double a garden's perceived size.

Why Your Dog Always Seems to Know Exactly When You Need Them
Research from the University of Vienna has shown that dogs process their owners' emotional cues through brain regions closely analogous to those humans use for social bonding — which is why your dog so often materializes quietly beside you precisely when life feels heavy. Unlike nearly every other animal, dogs have co-evolved alongside humans for more than 15,000 years specifically to read faces, voices, and body language with remarkable accuracy. It is not magic, but it is close: a dog's companionship is one of the few forms of comfort that requires no explanation and asks absolutely nothing in return.
