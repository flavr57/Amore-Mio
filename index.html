NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI incentive — introduced in 2024 as the successor to the original NHR regime — offers a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-sourced income for qualifying new residents, with potential exemptions on certain categories of foreign income. Those in technology, scientific research, and highly qualified professions are well-positioned to benefit from the program's favorable terms.

Coax Your Bougainvillea Into One Last Glorious Flush Before Autumn
August is the ideal moment to nudge a well-established bougainvillea toward a final, spectacular bloom before the season turns — and the method is counterintuitively simple: mild, deliberate water stress. Reduce watering for seven to ten days, let the soil dry more thoroughly between drinks, and the plant responds by channeling energy into flowers rather than foliage. Pair this with a low-nitrogen, high-phosphorus fertilizer and you may be rewarded with the most vivid display of the entire year.
