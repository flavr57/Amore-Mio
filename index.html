GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program continues in 2026 through qualifying routes including venture funds, cultural donations, and job creation — the direct residential property pathway has remained closed since 2023. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa remains an accessible parallel route for remote workers, with a modest income threshold and a clear five-year residency pathway.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto remain significantly pricier than just a few years ago, though rental price growth has moderated in 2026 compared to the sharp rises of 2022–2024. The Silver Coast and interior Alentejo continue to offer compelling value for those willing to live a little off the well-worn expat trail.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the successor to the original NHR program — offers qualifying new residents a favorable 20% flat tax on Portuguese-sourced income for up to ten years, targeting professions in tech, science, and the arts. Early consultation with a Portuguese tax specialist is strongly advised to confirm eligibility and register within the required window after establishing residency.

HOUSING MARKET
Property prices in the Algarve and Greater Lisbon remain elevated as international demand stays firm heading into the summer season. Rents have shown modest softening in some Lisbon neighborhoods, but quality two-bedroom apartments in desirable areas still command premium prices — patience and local contacts remain your best assets.

Nasturtiums: The Edible Flame-Colored Flower That Asks Almost Nothing
Few plants repay minimal effort as generously as nasturtiums — cascading blooms in fiery oranges, scarlets, and golds that tumble over containers and borders from early summer through autumn without complaint. Both the flowers and the peppery leaves are edible, equally lovely scattered over a salad or simply left to blaze in the garden. Sow directly where they are to grow in late spring, in poor soil with full sun, and then largely ignore them: they thrive on slight neglect.
