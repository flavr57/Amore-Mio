Portugal's replacement for the original NHR scheme — the IFICI incentive introduced in 2024 — offers qualifying new residents a reduced 20% flat tax on Portuguese-sourced income for up to ten years. Bougainvillea Blooms Best Under Stress — Unlock Its Full Colour With These Tips
One of the most rewarding plants for a warm-climate garden, bougainvillea puts on its most spectacular show when treated with a little benign neglect: lean soil, strong direct sun, and modest watering once established are all it truly needs. For a future garden in Portugal, where long dry summers and bright skies suit it perfectly, plant it against a south-facing wall, hold back on nitrogen-heavy fertiliser (which feeds leaves at the expense of those glorious papery bracts), and give it a hard prune after the main flush to encourage a second wave of colour before autumn. The reward for this minimal effort is a cascade of magenta, coral, or white that stops passers-by in their tracks.
