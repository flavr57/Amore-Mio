GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa continues to attract international applicants through qualifying fund investments and other non-real-estate pathways, even after the property route closed in 2023. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa remains a popular parallel option for remote workers, with a clear pathway toward long-term residency.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen meaningful increases in dining, groceries, and daily expenses over the past two years, though Portugal remains significantly more affordable than most Western European capitals. A comfortable lifestyle for a couple runs roughly €2,500–3,500 per month in Lisbon — less in the Algarve's smaller towns.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's original NHR program closed to new applicants in January 2024 and was replaced by the IFICI regime, which offers a 20% flat tax on Portuguese-source income for qualifying professionals and researchers for up to ten years. A Portuguese tax advisor can help confirm eligibility under the new framework before your move.

HOUSING MARKET
Property prices in Lisbon and the Algarve remain elevated with strong international demand, though the pace of increase has moderated in 2026. Long-term rental supply is gradually improving as short-term rental regulations tighten, giving arriving expats a bit more room to shop carefully.

dolce far niente
/DOHL-cheh far NYEN-teh/
the sweetness of doing nothing (noun phrase)
Dopo pranzo, ci siamo seduti sul terrazzo a goderci il dolce far niente.
Pull it apart: 'dolce' means sweet — think Dolce & Gabbana dripping in luxury. 'Far' means to do. 'Niente' is nothing — like 'nada,' with an Italian accent. Put it together: the sweetness of doing absolutely nothing. Italy turned idleness into an art form and gave it a name.

Bougainvillea's Secret: Why a Little Summer Stress Triggers More Blooms
Bougainvillea is one of the great paradoxes of the plant world: it blooms most brilliantly when you hold back water and crowd its roots. Those vivid bracts that look like flowers are actually modified leaves, triggered by mild stress — so if yours is sulking in an oversized pot with generous irrigation, try root-binding it and letting the soil dry out completely between waterings. In Hermosa Beach's warm coastal summer, this is exactly the right moment to pull back and let the plant do its spectacular, color-saturated thing.
