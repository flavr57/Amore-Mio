Lisbon and Porto have seen steady cost increases over recent years, with dining, groceries, and utilities all creeping upward — though both cities remain meaningfully more affordable than comparable Western European capitals. NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's updated tax incentive, now known as IFICI (Incentivo Fiscal à Criação de Valor), offers eligible new residents a preferential 20% flat rate on qualifying Portuguese-source income for up to ten years. Foreign pension and passive investment income may also receive favorable treatment under the scheme, making it worth a close look with a qualified tax adviser.

For the first time since medieval Europe, Italy has more woodland than agricultural land — forests now stretch across 60,000 square miles of the peninsula.
