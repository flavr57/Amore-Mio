GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa remains open and active in 2026, with investment fund routes and qualifying real estate in low-density regions proving most popular among American applicants. Application volumes have stabilized after the 2023 reforms, and the path to residency — and eventual citizenship — remains among Europe's most accessible for non-EU nationals.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto continue their gradual upward drift in dining, services, and everyday costs, though both cities remain meaningfully more affordable than comparable Western European capitals. Those willing to explore the Silver Coast, the Alentejo, or the interior Algarve will find their money stretching considerably further — and the quality of life is hard to argue with.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's new IFICI program — the refined successor to the original NHR — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-sourced income and broad exemptions on most foreign-source income for up to 10 years. For those arriving from high-tax states like California, the long-term savings can be genuinely life-changing.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental inventory in Lisbon and Porto remains tight, with demand from both locals and international arrivals keeping prices firm in the most popular neighborhoods. The emerging strategy among savvy movers: spend a few months in a furnished short-term rental to properly explore before committing — the right neighborhood makes all the difference.
