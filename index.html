NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's new IFICI regime — the successor to NHR — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-sourced income and potential exemptions on certain foreign income for a 10-year period. American expats planning ahead should consult a cross-border tax specialist, as US-Portugal treaty nuances require careful navigation.

Time to Prune Your Early Flowering Shrubs — Here's How
If your garden has shrubs like forsythia, lilac, or flowering currant that have just finished blooming, now is the ideal window to prune them — cutting back just after flowering allows them to set next year's buds on fresh new growth. The key is to remove spent flowering stems back to a healthy outward-facing bud or shoot, avoiding the temptation to shear everything uniformly. A little thoughtful pruning now means a far more abundant and shapely display when they bloom again next spring.
