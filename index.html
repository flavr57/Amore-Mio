Ninety Years Ago Today, Jesse Owens Made Olympic History in Berlin
On August 9, 1936, Jesse Owens claimed his fourth gold medal at the Berlin Olympics — a first for any American track-and-field athlete — in an arena Hitler had designed as a stage for racial ideology. His victories in the 100m, 200m, long jump, and 4×100m relay arrived as a quiet, definitive argument for what human beings are actually capable of, and the German crowd cheered him anyway. Ninety years on, the image of a young man from Ohio outrunning an entire worldview still lifts the heart.

GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains open for qualifying fund investments, cultural contributions, and job-creation ventures following the 2023 closure of the residential property route. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to offer a clear pathway for remote workers earning above the Portuguese minimum-wage threshold, with processing times stabilising in 2026.

COST OF LIVING
Groceries, dining, and transport in Lisbon run roughly 35–45% below comparable Southern California costs, though rents in central neighbourhoods have climbed steadily and no longer represent the bargain they once did. The Algarve and secondary cities like Braga, Setúbal, and Évora continue to offer meaningfully better value — and a noticeably gentler pace.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI scheme — the successor to the now-closed NHR — offers eligible new residents a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-source employment income and potential exemptions on qualifying foreign income for up to ten years. The programme continues to attract pensioners, remote workers, and high-value professionals, with 2025 and 2026 arrivals actively applying.

HOUSING MARKET
Central Lisbon and Porto one-bedrooms are averaging €1,300–€1,900 per month, with the government's Mais Habitação rent controls providing modest relief in some areas but not yet reversing the broader upward trend. Expats are increasingly finding their best quality-of-life-per-euro ratio in coastal towns between Lisbon and the Algarve — places like Setúbal, Comporta, and Vila Nova de Milfontes.

Italian authorities have announced the discovery of an ancient Roman shipwreck off the coast of Sicily that Italy's culture minister is calling one of the most important underwater archaeological finds in years. Excavations are expected to surface artifacts that could reframe scholarly understanding of the period's seafaring culture.

Georgia's Loggerhead Sea Turtles Set a 37-Year Nesting Record This Season
Loggerhead sea turtles are nesting along Georgia's Atlantic coastline in numbers not seen since 1989 — a new 37-year record that researchers are calling a genuine conservation milestone. The surge is the result of decades of patient work: protected nesting beaches, reduced bycatch in commercial fishing gear, and dimmed coastal lighting that once disoriented hatchlings making their first run to the sea. It is a reminder, offered every summer in the form of tiny turtles, that when humans choose to make room for other species, nature tends to respond with extraordinary generosity.

If your bougainvillea is blazing right now — as it should be on a warm Hermosa August — the counterintuitive secret to extending that colour through autumn is to hold back: ease up on watering and skip nitrogen-heavy fertiliser, which pushes lush leafy growth at the expense of those vivid bracts. A deep weekly soak followed by several dry days mimics the plant's native Brazilian dry season and reliably triggers a fresh flush of bloom. In Portugal, where bougainvillea drapes entire whitewashed hillsides from spring through late autumn, gardeners swear by exactly this rhythm — and your plant is already primed to follow it.
