GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa continues under its restructured framework, with investment routes now centered on capital transfer, job creation, and cultural or scientific activities rather than urban real estate. It remains one of Europe's most accessible pathways to EU residency for non-EU nationals.
~
COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto rents remain elevated by Southern European standards, though price growth has moderated after years of sharp rises. The Silver Coast, Alentejo, and interior cities offer considerably more value for those willing to live beyond the capital.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — successor to the original NHR — offers a 20% flat tax on Portuguese-source income for qualifying new residents over a 10-year period, a compelling advantage for retirees and remote workers relocating from high-tax countries.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Property prices in Lisbon have plateaued after years of rapid appreciation, though international buyer demand remains firm in Cascais, Sintra, and the Algarve. Rental inventory in central Lisbon is still tight — a local agent with access to off-market listings is worth their weight.

Bougainvillea's Secret to Maximum Blooms: A Little Deliberate Neglect
If your bougainvillea is producing all foliage and little color, the culprit is likely too much water or too much nitrogen — this sun-loving climber blooms most extravagantly when gently stressed. In Hermosa Beach's climate (and equally in Portugal's Algarve, where you're headed), the formula is elegantly simple: full sun, lean well-draining soil, and deep but infrequent watering once established. Counterintuitively, a little benign neglect is the kindest thing you can offer it.

Three Bedroom Plants That Quietly Clean the Air While You Sleep
Snake plants (Sansevieria), peace lilies, and spider plants are the unsung heroes of indoor air quality — filtering trace toxins and releasing oxygen through the night without any fuss. Snake plants in particular thrive in low light and forgive irregular watering, making them ideal for a bedroom corner that catches only morning sun. For a busy household, these plants ask almost nothing and give back quietly, every single night.
