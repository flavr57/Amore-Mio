NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the streamlined successor to the original NHR — offers new qualifying residents a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-source professional income and broad exemptions on eligible foreign income for a full ten years. For retirees and location-independent professionals, it remains one of the most compelling fiscal frameworks available anywhere in the European Union.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental inventory in central Lisbon and Porto stays tight, with monthly rents in desirable neighborhoods pushing firmly upward — though buyers in the €300k–€500k range are finding slightly more negotiating room than in the peak years. Looking inland to Coimbra, Évora, or the smaller Algarve towns unlocks considerably more space, charm, and value for the same budget.
