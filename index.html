GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa remains available through qualifying investment funds, venture capital, and job-creation routes — direct real estate purchases are no longer eligible in most categories. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to attract remote workers worldwide, requiring proof of income roughly four times the national minimum wage (approximately €3,480/month in 2026).

COST OF LIVING
Everyday costs in Lisbon and Porto have climbed meaningfully over the past few years, though Portugal still offers real savings compared to London, Paris, or California. The Algarve remains more affordable outside peak tourist season, with groceries and dining notably lighter on the wallet than Hermosa Beach standards.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
The original NHR programme closed to new applicants in early 2024 and was replaced by IFICI — a regime offering a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-sourced income for qualifying professionals in strategic sectors, plus broad exemptions on most foreign-source income. New arrivals should explore IFICI carefully with a local tax adviser to confirm eligibility.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in central Lisbon and Porto remain elevated, with one-bedroom apartments in prime neighbourhoods averaging €1,200–€1,800 per month. The market shows signs of stabilisation outside the capitals — inland gems like Évora and Coimbra continue to offer exceptional quality of life at a significant discount.

A 1936 Treaty Still Quietly Shapes Power in the Black Sea
The Montreux Convention — signed ninety years ago to settle a regional dispute few now remember — grants Turkey sovereign control over which warships may transit the Bosphorus and Dardanelles, effectively limiting NATO's ability to project naval power into the Black Sea during the Ukraine war. The treaty was designed for a world that no longer exists, yet it remains binding international law. As maritime conflict around Odesa intensifies, this peculiar document's durability is drawing fresh scrutiny from strategists and historians alike.

Biotech Brings the American Chestnut Back From a Century of Exile
A blight fungus arrived in North America around 1900 and, within decades, had wiped out nearly four billion American chestnut trees — one of the great ecological catastrophes of the modern era. Now a biotech startup, wielding big data and genomic selection, has bred a generation of 'elite' blight-resistant chestnuts with no detectable genetic defects, moving the species meaningfully closer to restoration across its native range. It is the kind of patient, science-driven optimism that makes the future feel genuinely worth leaning into.

Coaxing Peak Bloom from Your Bougainvillea This Summer
Bougainvillea thrives on strategic tough love: once new growth appears in summer, reduce watering and allow the soil to dry out between drinks — mild stress is precisely what triggers that spectacular bract display. A high-potassium fertiliser (a tomato or bloom formula works perfectly) applied every two weeks through July and August deepens the colour considerably. In coastal climates like Hermosa Beach — and indeed the Algarve — a minimum of six full hours of direct sun daily is non-negotiable for truly exuberant blooms.
