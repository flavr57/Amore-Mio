GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa continues under its revised framework, with real estate purchases no longer qualifying — eligible routes now center on investment funds, job creation, and cultural or scientific activities. Processing times remain lengthy, so anyone eyeing a 2026–2027 move would do well to begin the paperwork now.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto remain meaningfully more affordable than comparable Western European capitals, though both cities have seen sustained price pressure over the past three years. Day-to-day expenses in Portugal still run roughly 30–40% below California, and the Silver Coast and interior Alentejo offer even greater breathing room.

NHR TAX REGIME
The original NHR regime closed to new applicants in January 2024 but was succeeded by IFICI — a targeted incentive offering reduced flat tax rates for qualifying professionals in technology, science, and innovation. Expats with the right professional profile can still access significant tax advantages as new Portuguese residents.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in central Lisbon and Porto have stabilized somewhat following 2023's Mais Habitação legislation, but expat and digital-nomad demand keeps the market competitive. Looking slightly outside city centres — or toward Setúbal, Braga, or the Alentejo — unlocks considerably better value for the same budget.

Bougainvillea Blooms Best When You Step Back and Let It Be
Bougainvillea is one of the great gifts of a Mediterranean climate: a plant that rewards tough love with cascades of magenta, coral, and white. The key is resisting the urge to overwater — allow the soil to dry out fully between waterings, as wet roots suppress flowering and invite rot far more reliably than drought does. Once established in a warm, sunny spot, a well-sited bougainvillea will bloom nearly year-round with minimal fuss, making it an ideal anchor for a Hermosa Beach patio or a future Portuguese courtyard.
