NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the successor to the original NHR — offers qualifying new residents a 20% flat tax on Portuguese-sourced income for up to ten years, with foreign pension and passive investment income potentially exempt under treaty conditions. Early consultation with a Portuguese fiscal adviser before your move remains the single most valuable step you can take.

Bougainvillea in Late Summer: Coaxing One Final Spectacular Flush of Color
August is the moment to encourage a bougainvillea into its most dramatic late-season display — the secret is a deliberate period of water stress followed by a generous deep soak, which signals the plant to redirect energy from foliage into flower. Once the papery bracts begin appearing, ease back on nitrogen-heavy fertiliser and switch to a high-potassium feed to intensify and sustain the color.
