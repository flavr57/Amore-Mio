NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's updated IFICI regime — successor to the original NHR — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-sourced income for up to ten years, one of Europe's most attractive arrangements for incoming professionals and retirees. Registration through a local tax advisor remains the recommended first step before arriving.

As airfares climb and highway gridlock grinds, more US travelers are rediscovering trains — only to encounter a network built almost entirely around freight, not people. The contrast with Europe and Asia is stark: Japan's Shinkansen and France's TGV were engineered from the outset as passenger systems, while Amtrak must share tracks on timetables it does not control. Bougainvillea Blooms Most Brilliantly When You Trust Its Dry Spells
Bougainvillea rewards what feels counterintuitive: the less water you give it once established, the more intensely it blooms. In Portugal's warm, dry summers that is a genuine gift — the plant's natural drought stress is precisely what triggers those explosions of magenta, coral, and white bracts.
