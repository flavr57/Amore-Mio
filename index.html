When Idle Cash Can Quietly Earn Its Keep
SGOV and BIL are exchange-traded funds that hold ultra-short-dated US Treasury bills — the same instruments the federal government issues for one to three months at a time, now packaged so anyone with a brokerage account can buy in as easily as a stock. In higher interest-rate environments, funds like these have historically delivered yields broadly in line with prevailing short-term rates — running somewhere in the 4–5% neighborhood during elevated-rate periods, though that range has swung from near zero to well above 5% across different cycles. The mechanism is elegantly simple: each fund continuously rolls maturing T-bills into new ones at current rates, passing the interest to shareholders as monthly distributions, all backed by the full faith and credit of the US government. The honest trade-off is that these funds track the federal funds rate downward just as readily as up — when the Fed cuts, your yield follows, and there is no lock-in to cushion you. A productive next thread: compare SGOV (iShares, BlackRock) directly against BIL (SPDR, State Street) on expense ratios and average maturity, then pull up USFR from WisdomTree — a floating-rate Treasury ETF that handles duration a little differently — to understand how the three behave across rate cycles. TikTok's $400 Million Settlement Marks a Turning Point for Children's Digital Privacy
TikTok and its parent company ByteDance have agreed to pay $400 million to resolve a lawsuit alleging that the platform systematically collected vast amounts of data on millions of users under the age of thirteen — one of the largest child privacy settlements on record. The case reflects a broader reckoning with how platforms engineered around attention and engagement have treated their youngest users, often well ahead of any legal guardrails meant to protect them. For parents, regulators, and anyone watching the evolution of digital rights, it signals that the era of consequence-free data harvesting from children may finally be drawing to a close.

Africa's Largest Renewable Energy Plant Is Now Powering 200,000 Homes
TotalEnergies has inaugurated what is now Africa's largest hybrid renewable energy project — a facility capable of generating 400 gigawatt-hours of electricity by pairing solar generation with utility-scale battery storage, bringing reliable clean power to roughly 200,000 households. The storage component directly addresses one of renewable energy's oldest challenges: what happens after sunset or on overcast days, which has long made communities hesitant to depend on solar alone. It is the kind of infrastructure milestone that tends to compound — once the lights are reliably on, everything else becomes possible.

Bougainvillea's Secret: A Little Stress Makes for a Much Better Show
Bougainvillea is one of those plants that rewards a certain studied neglect — when mildly stressed by reduced watering, it redirects energy away from leafy growth and into the vivid, papery bracts that make it one of the most dramatic plants in any warm-climate garden. Letting the soil dry out noticeably between waterings, rather than keeping it consistently moist, is the single most reliable way to trigger a spectacular bloom cycle, particularly in containers. If yours has been looking lush but flower-shy, try pulling back irrigation for two or three weeks and watching what happens.

Therapy Dogs in Libraries Are Turning Reluctant Readers Into Devoted Ones
Library systems across the country have quietly built one of the most effective reading interventions imaginable: a child, a stack of books, and a calm dog who listens without judgment or correction.
