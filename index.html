GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains active for fund investments, scientific research, and cultural contributions — the property route in high-density areas was closed in 2023, but multiple qualifying pathways remain open. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to attract remote workers, with a straightforward process for those meeting the income threshold.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen steady price increases over the past two years, with groceries and dining now noticeably pricier than in 2022 — though still well below London or Paris. The Algarve and the Silver Coast remain the best-value sweet spots for expats seeking sun without the urban premium.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal replaced the original NHR scheme with IFICI (Incentivo Fiscal à Captação de Investimento) starting January 2024, offering a 20% flat tax on Portuguese-source professional income for eligible new residents over a 10-year period. Qualifying categories include tech workers, researchers, and highly qualified professionals — a Portuguese tax advisor can confirm your eligibility quickly.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental demand in Lisbon continues to outpace supply, keeping prices elevated — a well-located one-bedroom in the city center averages €1,500–1,800 per month. The market is considerably more forgiving in secondary cities like Braga, Setúbal, and the Alentejo, where quality rentals can be found at roughly half the Lisbon price.

International health experts convened in Laos this summer to mark what few dared predict two decades ago: Southeast Asia has reduced malaria cases by more than two-thirds since 2010 and is now approaching elimination. It is one of the quiet triumphs of global public health, built on persistent effort, community health workers, and sustained political will.
