Lisbon and Porto remain meaningfully more affordable than coastal California, though local prices have risen steadily over the past three years, particularly for dining and services. NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's new IFICI incentive — the successor to the original NHR program — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% income tax rate for up to ten years on Portuguese-source income. Professionals in technology, science, the arts, and other designated fields are among those eligible, making it one of Europe's more attractive fiscal frameworks for relocating creatives and knowledge workers.

Two Hundred and Fifty Years Ago Today, America First Put Rights Into Writing
On June 12, 1776, Virginia's colonial legislature became the first governing body in the Americas to formally adopt a Bill of Rights — the Virginia Declaration of Rights, drafted by George Mason. It declared that all people are born free and equal, and directly seeded both the U.S. Bill of Rights and France's Declaration of the Rights of Man. It is a quiet anniversary for one of the most consequential paragraphs in the long, still-unfinished story of human liberty.

Bougainvillea's Secret: A Little Thirst Unlocks the Brightest Blooms
Bougainvillea is one of the few plants that genuinely rewards a touch of neglect — slightly stressed roots in fast-draining soil, with full sun and infrequent but deep watering, will produce far more vivid color than pampered ones. The trick is to let the soil dry out almost completely between waterings, which triggers the plant's flowering impulse rather than leafy vegetative growth. In Hermosa Beach's warm, sunny climate, a south- or west-facing wall is all the encouragement it needs to put on a spectacular summer show.
