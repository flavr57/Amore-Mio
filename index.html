Twice-Yearly HIV Injection Shows Zero New Infections in Major Trial
A twice-yearly injection of lenacapavir has delivered what scientists are calling near-miraculous results: zero new HIV infections among 3,200 trial participants across two stages of testing. The findings mark what could be a genuine turning point in the decades-long fight against HIV/AIDS, offering a path where prevention requires nothing more than two doctor's visits a year. For millions of people in high-risk communities worldwide, a simple biannual shot may soon replace the burden of daily medication — and that changes everything.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's revamped IFICI scheme — which replaced the original NHR program in 2024 — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-source income for up to ten years. Americans should work with a cross-border tax advisor early, as US worldwide taxation rules add a layer of complexity worth addressing before the move.

One Ice Cube a Week Is All Your Orchid Needs to Thrive
Phalaenopsis orchids — the graceful arching blooms most of us bring home from the grocery store — ask for surprisingly little: bright indirect light, a well-draining pot, and slow, even moisture delivered with restraint. One ice cube placed at the base weekly gives exactly the right amount of water without the root rot that kills more orchids than drought ever does. As June warms, move yours a foot back from a south-facing window and watch the roots turn a healthy silver-green — the orchid's quiet sign of contentment.

Nasturtiums: The Cheerful Edible Blooms That Grow Best When Neglected
Few plants reward the gardener as generously as nasturtiums — quick from seed, vibrantly colored in orange, gold, and red, and entirely edible from flower to leaf to seed pod. Gardeners World's expert guide recommends sowing them directly into poor-to-average soil, because too much richness pushes leafy green growth at the expense of those glorious blooms. Their peppery flowers make a stunning garnish and their trailing habit softens the edges of containers and raised beds with an almost effortless grace.
