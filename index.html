A Native Tribe Is One Signature Away From Reclaiming Its River
Twenty years after the demolition of the Elwha River dams in Washington State, the Lower Elwha Klallam Tribe stands just one signature away from reclaiming 1,100 acres of restored ancestral land along the once-submerged riverbanks. The ecosystem has been quietly healing itself — salmon have returned, willows are taking root, and a landscape that lay buried under a reservoir for a century is becoming recognizable again. It is one of the most quietly extraordinary environmental restoration stories of the decade.

The SEP IRA: Retirement Shelter Built for the Solo Business Owner
A SEP IRA — Simplified Employee Pension Individual Retirement Account — is a tax-deferred retirement account the IRS designed specifically for self-employed people and small business owners, and it is about as close to effortless as retirement planning gets. Contribution limits have historically been on the generous end: the ceiling has generally run around 25% of your net self-employment compensation up to a substantial annual cap the IRS adjusts periodically, so think in rough magnitudes rather than precise figures. The mechanism is clean — contributions reduce your taxable income dollar-for-dollar in the year you make them, the money compounds tax-deferred inside the account, and you can open one at virtually any brokerage in an afternoon with minimal paperwork. The honest watch-out is the employee parity rule: if you have staff, the SEP requires you to contribute the same percentage of compensation for them as you do for yourself, which changes the math considerably as payroll grows. If your teenagers are doing legitimate, documented work for your business, it is worth a conversation with a CPA about whether putting them on payroll could open its own planning possibilities — and speaking of CPAs, this is exactly the kind of territory where one should confirm the specifics before you act, because these rules do shift and the details carry real weight. To go deeper, pull up IRS Publication 560 (Retirement Plans for Small Business) and Form 5305-SEP — those two documents lay out the whole framework. ESA's New Sentinel 3C Satellite Returns Its First Sumptuous Images of Earth
Just two weeks after its launch, the European Space Agency's Copernicus Sentinel 3C satellite has returned its debut images — sweeping, high-resolution views of Italy's coastline and the Arctic's ice fields that scientists are calling remarkable in their clarity and detail. The Sentinel series is the backbone of Europe's Earth observation program, feeding the continuous, systematic climate data researchers depend on to track everything from sea surface temperatures to forest loss. These first images are a quiet reminder that one of the genuinely great ongoing achievements of our era is the fleet of attentive eyes we have placed in orbit simply to keep watch on our own planet.

October Is the Moment to Pull Back on Watering Your Bougainvillea
As days shorten and temperatures ease — even modestly along the Southern California coast — bougainvillea begins its natural transition toward dormancy, and the most common mistake this time of year is continuing to water as freely as summer demanded. Pulling back on irrigation now, letting the soil dry more thoroughly between waterings, signals the plant to shift energy toward setting flower buds rather than pushing new leafy growth. A slightly water-stressed bougainvillea in October is, reliably, a spectacularly blooming one come spring.

Why Dogs Know When You Are Sad — and What They Actually Do About It
Research in canine cognition has made it increasingly clear that dogs don't merely sense human emotional states — they orient toward distress in the people they love in a way that looks, by every measurable marker, like genuine concern. Studies using behavioral observation and physiological monitoring have shown that dogs will interrupt what they are doing to approach and make physical contact with a crying or visibly distressed person, even when doing so takes effort. It is, as one researcher put it, the oldest service-animal relationship in the world — still working exactly as designed after fifteen thousand years.
