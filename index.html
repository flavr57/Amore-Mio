Ancient 'Miracle Tree' Seeds Filter 98% of Microplastics From Water
Seeds from the Moringa oleifera tree — revered in Ayurvedic and African traditional medicine for millennia — have been shown to remove more than 98% of microplastics from tap water. Researchers found that natural proteins in the seeds bind to plastic particles, causing them to clump and settle out of the water. It's the kind of solution that feels almost too elegant: a tree that has fed and healed communities for thousands of years, now quietly offering to clean up one of modernity's most stubborn messes.

GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program continues to accept applications through qualifying investment routes — including venture capital funds and artistic production — following the removal of direct real estate as an eligible category. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa remains a strong option for remote workers, requiring proof of income at roughly four times Portugal's minimum wage (currently around €3,480/month).

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto continue to see gradual price increases in dining, groceries, and services as expat and tourism demand remains elevated, though both cities still offer meaningful savings compared to California. The Algarve and smaller interior cities like Évora or Braga remain notably more affordable for day-to-day living.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's new IFICI regime (the successor to the original NHR program) offers a flat 20% income tax rate for qualifying new residents — including many remote workers and retirees — for a period of ten years. Those who registered under the original NHR before its 2024 closure continue to enjoy their existing benefits through the end of their ten-year window.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Property prices in Lisbon and the Algarve remain at or near historic highs, with rental availability in central neighborhoods still tight heading into summer 2026. Buyers and renters are increasingly finding better value by looking just outside city centers — neighborhoods like Almada, Setúbal, or the Silver Coast offer quality of life at a noticeably gentler price point.
