Greece Signs Law Protecting Last Refuge of Mediterranean Monk Seals
The President of Greece recently signed into law a new national marine protected area around Gyaros — a remote, uninhabited island that serves as critical habitat for the endangered Mediterranean monk seal. With fewer than 800 of these seals remaining in the wild, the protected zone offers a genuine lifeline for one of the ocean's rarest mammals. Conservation groups hailed it as a landmark moment for Aegean biodiversity.

GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains active for qualifying investments — including venture capital funds, cultural projects, and job creation — following the 2023 reforms that removed direct residential real estate as an eligible category. Processing times have improved through 2026, making it a more predictable path to EU residency than in recent years.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto remain far more affordable than California overall, though popular expat neighborhoods like Príncipe Real and Bonfim have seen steady rent increases. Groceries, restaurants, and daily life still offer excellent value — the main pressure point is central-city housing, where demand from both locals and new arrivals continues to outpace supply.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's updated IFICI incentive — successor to the original NHR — offers qualifying new residents a 20% flat tax on Portuguese-source professional income and favorable treatment of many foreign-income streams for up to ten years. Americans planning the move should work with a cross-border tax specialist early to optimize timing and structure before establishing residency.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in central Lisbon and Porto remain elevated, though new supply and government affordability initiatives are beginning to moderate growth in some neighborhoods. Areas along the Silver Coast and around Setúbal continue to offer more accessible pricing — and increasingly, genuinely beautiful alternatives to the big-city bustle.

The Counterintuitive Secret to Getting Bougainvillea to Bloom Brilliantly
If your bougainvillea has been all leaf and no flower, the fix is deliberately counterintuitive: stress it. Pulling back on watering for two to three weeks triggers the plant's survival mode, which is precisely what pushes it into spectacular bloom. Once the papery bracts begin to show color, resume normal watering and switch to a low-nitrogen, high-phosphorus fertilizer to sustain the display through the warm months ahead.
