How to Keep Bougainvillea Blooming Brilliantly Through the Rest of Summer
Bougainvillea thrives on a little tough love: it blooms most abundantly when slightly root-bound and kept on the drier side — overwatering is the single most common reason a healthy plant abruptly stops flowering. In late July, a light trim of any leggy, non-blooming stems encourages a fresh flush of color before the season ends, and switching to a low-nitrogen, high-phosphorus fertilizer now will fuel that next round of brilliant bracts. If yours is container-grown, give it the sunniest spot available and resist repotting until roots are visibly escaping the drainage holes.
