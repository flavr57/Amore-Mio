GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa, restructured in 2023 to center on qualifying fund investments rather than direct real estate purchases, remains active and accessible. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa runs parallel as a strong pathway for remote workers, requiring demonstrated monthly income of at least €3,480.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen meaningful price increases since 2022, though both cities remain more affordable than comparable Western European capitals. Budget-conscious movers are finding the Silver Coast, Setúbal, and inland Alentejo increasingly compelling alternatives to the pricier urban centers.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the successor to the original Non-Habitual Resident program — offers a flat 20% rate on qualifying Portuguese-source income for up to ten years for new residents. American expats should coordinate with a US tax advisor given treaty considerations, but the framework remains one of Europe's most favorable for incoming professionals.

HOUSING MARKET
Portugal's rental market in Lisbon and Porto remains competitive, with desirable properties moving quickly and prices holding near recent highs. The Algarve and emerging coastal areas offer more breathing room, and new government housing initiatives may gradually ease urban supply constraints over the coming year.
