GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program, now channeled through investment funds and cultural contributions rather than direct real estate purchases, remains open and active for qualifying non-EU applicants. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to draw remote workers with a long-stay monthly income threshold of approximately €3,280 — a pathway that pairs beautifully with California-based remote income.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto remain meaningfully more affordable than comparable Western European capitals, though grocery, dining, and utility costs have crept upward through early 2026 on the back of broader European inflation. Those open to cities like Braga, Setúbal, or Évora will still find a genuinely lower cost of living without sacrificing charm or amenity.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI program — the successor to the original NHR — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-sourced income and broad exemptions on most foreign-source income for a ten-year window. New arrivals should register for fiscal residency promptly and apply within their first tax year to secure the benefit without complication.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in central Lisbon have plateaued somewhat after years of steep climbs, though available inventory in prime neighborhoods remains tight and well-priced flats move quickly. Prospective buyers may find more breathing room — and better negotiating leverage — in the Silver Coast, Alentejo, and smaller Algarve towns compared to the capital.

Stress Your Bougainvillea a Little and Watch It Reward You Spectacularly
Bougainvillea operates on a counterintuitive principle that experienced gardeners swear by: treat it too kindly and it sulks into green; stress it slightly and it erupts in color. Letting the soil dry out completely between waterings — rather than keeping it moist — signals the plant to redirect its energy from leafy growth into producing those brilliant, papery bracts. In Portugal's sunny, warm climate, a south-facing wall and a deliberately light watering hand will coax near year-round color from this Mediterranean icon.
