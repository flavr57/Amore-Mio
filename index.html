GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains open through investment fund routes, requiring a minimum commitment of €500,000, and continues to offer a clear pathway to residency and eventual EU citizenship. While the real estate route was closed in 2023, fund-based applications are active and processing times have steadily improved.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen meaningful price increases over recent years, though both cities remain notably more affordable than London, Paris, or Amsterdam. The Algarve offers a wide range of price points — coastal hotspots like Lagos carry a premium, while the Silver Coast and inland towns continue to offer genuinely excellent value.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's updated tax incentive for new residents — now called IFICI — provides a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-source professional income and favorable treatment of certain foreign-source income for qualifying individuals. Americans considering the move should work with a cross-border tax specialist early to optimize the structure from the outset.

HOUSING MARKET
Rental demand in Lisbon and Porto continues to outpace supply, keeping prices elevated and competition for well-located properties brisk. The silver lining: Portugal's interior regions and smaller coastal towns still offer spacious, character-rich homes at prices that feel almost impossible for anyone arriving from Southern California.

A punishing heatwave that swept western Europe has shifted east, with temperatures in Germany forecast to reach 40°C while France has raised its public health alert to the highest possible level — heat-related deaths are already being reported, including among young people. A new study across the European Union finds that one in seven teenagers now spends more than eight hours a day in front of screens, reigniting a sharp debate about whether social media bans are a meaningful solution or an oversimplification. Paris Museum Opens Gallery for Nazi-Looted Art Still Awaiting Its Rightful Owners
The Musée d'Orsay in Paris has done something quietly remarkable: it has opened a dedicated gallery for artworks stolen by the Nazis during World War II that have never been claimed by their original owners or their descendants. The museum's stated ambition is essentially to make the gallery redundant — curators hope that by placing these paintings in the light, surviving families across the world will finally be able to recognize and reclaim what was taken from them. It is an act of institutional humility and moral seriousness that the art world rarely sees, and a testament to the idea that history's debts do not simply expire with the passage of time.

Bougainvillea's Summer Secret: A Little Stress Brings Out the Best Blooms
Summer is bougainvillea's moment — those spectacular cascades of magenta, coral, and white that drape across walls from Hermosa Beach to the Algarve are at their most dramatic right now. The counterintuitive secret to keeping them blooming all season long is deliberate stress: let the soil dry out thoroughly between waterings and resist the urge to fertilize heavily with nitrogen, which pushes leafy growth at the expense of color. Full sun and excellent drainage do the rest — bougainvillea is one of those rare plants that genuinely thrives on benign neglect.
