GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's reformed Golden Visa program — which removed direct residential property as a qualifying investment in 2023 — continues to draw non-EU applicants through qualifying funds, cultural projects, and job creation pathways. Processing times have improved, and the D8 digital nomad visa remains an accessible parallel route for location-independent workers.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto have seen continued price creep in restaurants, groceries, and services, narrowing the gap with other Western European capitals — though both cities remain meaningfully more affordable than London or Paris. Smaller cities like Braga, Setúbal, and the Silver Coast corridor offer a noticeably gentler cost of living for those willing to venture beyond the obvious.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the 2024 successor to the beloved NHR — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-sourced income for a full decade, with foreign-sourced income from eligible categories remaining tax-exempt. For professionals, freelancers, and retirees, it remains one of Europe's most compelling fiscal welcomes.

Switzerland Votes on Capping Its Population at Ten Million People
Swiss voters are set to weigh in on a proposal from the right-wing Swiss People's Party to enshrine a 10-million population ceiling in the constitution — framed as a sustainability initiative but opposed by critics who warn it would require aggressive immigration restrictions and could destabilize the country's economy and international relationships. The referendum reflects a tension playing out across prosperous small democracies: how to reconcile openness and cosmopolitanism with anxieties about density, belonging, and change.

French Polynesia Shields 200,000 Square Miles of Vibrant Ocean From Harm
The French Polynesian government has announced full protection for 200,000 square miles of biodiverse ocean — an area roughly twice the size of Arizona — in one of the most expansive marine conservation declarations of the decade. The zone encompasses some of the Pacific's most intact coral ecosystems, critical shark and whale migration corridors, and thousands of species found nowhere else on Earth. At a moment when ocean warming and acidification threaten reefs worldwide, this is the kind of bold, preemptive act of stewardship that gives the conservation community genuine cause for hope.

Therapy Dogs in Schools Are Quietly Transforming How Children Learn to Read
In a growing number of schools across the US and Europe, certified therapy dogs sit patiently as children read aloud to them — and the results are remarkable: kids read longer, stumble less, and report far lower anxiety than when reading to adults. The logic is simple and beautiful: a dog offers pure, non-judgmental presence, and a child who trips over a word receives nothing in return but a warm, unhurried gaze. Programs like R.E.A.D. (Reading Education Assistance Dogs) have now reached hundreds of schools, a quiet reminder that sometimes the most powerful learning tool in the room has four paws and a wagging tail.
