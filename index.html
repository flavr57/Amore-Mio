GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains open and active in 2026, with qualifying routes now centered on fund investments and urban rehabilitation projects rather than direct residential property. AIMA has improved processing timelines for complete applications, making the path to residency more predictable for well-prepared applicants.

COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto remain meaningfully more affordable than comparable Western European capitals, though grocery and dining costs have risen roughly 4–6% year-over-year. Those willing to explore mid-sized cities like Braga, Évora, or the Silver Coast will find day-to-day expenses considerably gentler on the wallet.

NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI regime — the NHR successor — continues to offer a flat 20% rate on Portuguese-sourced income for qualifying new residents, with favorable treatment of foreign pension and certain investment income for up to ten years. It remains one of Europe's most attractive tax incentive packages for relocating professionals and retirees alike.

Rental availability in central Lisbon and Porto stays tight despite government stabilization measures, but expats are discovering genuine value in neighborhoods just outside the historic cores and in smaller coastal towns. The Secret to Bougainvillea's Most Brilliant Color Is Intentional Stress
Bougainvillea's spectacular display comes not from its tiny white flowers but from the vivid papery bracts surrounding them — and the key to keeping those bracts luminous is a counterintuitive technique: let the plant struggle a little. Water deeply but infrequently, allow the soil to dry out between sessions, and resist overfeeding with nitrogen, which drives leafy growth at the expense of color. Full sun, occasional drought, and a phosphorus-rich feed every few weeks is the formula for a bougainvillea that stops passersby in their tracks.
