GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program remains open in 2026, now centered on investment in qualifying funds, cultural heritage projects, and job creation — the real estate route was closed in 2023. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa continues to offer a practical residency pathway for remote workers earning above roughly €3,280/month.
~
COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto remain meaningfully more affordable than London or Paris, but central rents have continued rising — expect €1,500–2,000/month for a one-bedroom in Lisbon's prime neighborhoods. Cities like Braga, Setúbal, and the Silver Coast still offer genuine value for those willing to look beyond the headline destinations.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's IFICI scheme — the 2024 successor to the beloved NHR — offers qualifying new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-sourced income and broad exemptions on foreign income for up to ten years. It remains one of Europe's most attractive fiscal frameworks for internationally mobile professionals and creatives.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Property prices in Lisbon are holding at roughly €4,500–6,000 per square meter, with the rental market still tight in most central neighborhoods. Government housing initiatives and new construction are gradually adding supply, but patience and a wide geographic search remain the wisest strategy for incoming residents.

Bougainvillea: The Sun-Drunk Showstopper That Blooms More When You Ignore It
Few plants make a bolder statement with less fuss than bougainvillea, and for anyone moving toward a sunnier, Mediterranean climate — say, southern Portugal — it will become your great garden ally. Plant it in the hottest spot available, give it lean soil (it blooms more extravagantly when slightly stressed), and water deeply but infrequently once established. Those spectacular papery bracts — which are modified leaves, not petals — will reward your restraint with months of cascading color from coral to magenta.
