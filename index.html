GOLDEN VISA
Portugal's Golden Visa program continues to accept applications focused on fund investments, cultural contributions, and job creation — real estate purchases remain excluded in most categories. The D8 Digital Nomad Visa remains a popular alternative for remote workers, requiring proof of steady income above roughly four times Portugal's minimum wage.
~
COST OF LIVING
Lisbon and Porto continue to see upward pressure on everyday costs, with dining and groceries creeping closer to Western European norms, though still meaningfully below California prices. The Algarve and smaller inland towns remain considerably more affordable and are attracting a growing wave of quality-conscious expats.
✓
NHR TAX REGIME
Portugal's updated IFICI regime — the successor to the original NHR — offers new residents a flat 20% tax rate on Portuguese-source income and potential exemptions on certain foreign income for a 10-year period. Prospective movers are encouraged to apply in the year they establish residency to lock in the benefit.
~
HOUSING MARKET
Rental prices in Lisbon's historic neighborhoods have stabilized somewhat after years of sharp increases, though supply remains tight and competition for quality long-term rentals is fierce. Buying property continues to offer strong long-term value, particularly in the Silver Coast and Alentejo regions where prices remain more accessible.
